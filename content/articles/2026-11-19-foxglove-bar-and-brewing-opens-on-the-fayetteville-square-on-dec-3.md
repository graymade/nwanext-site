Fayetteville, the Square is getting a new brewery. 🍻

Foxglove Bar and Brewing opens Thursday, Dec 3rd, right on the Square at 37 E Center St. Expect house-brewed beer and crafted cocktails, all in the old Hofbrau space, original floor and all.

📍 Foxglove Bar and Brewing, 37 E Center St, Fayetteville
🗓️ Thursday, Dec 3rd, 3 to 10 PM

It opens the same night as the Lights of the Ozarks Parade, so the Square will be buzzing. Come say hi to Logan and Hugh.
