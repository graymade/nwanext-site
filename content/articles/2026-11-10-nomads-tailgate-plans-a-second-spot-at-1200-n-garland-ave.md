Fayetteville, Nomads Tailgate is coming back with a second spot, and this one is right off campus.

The team just signed the lease on the former Waystone Pizza space at 1200 N Garland Ave, and they are aiming to open in early February. Expect the same live music community, plus a brand new pub-style menu built for game days: burgers, fish n chips, chicken tenders, breakfast, and a full bar.

📍 1200 N Garland Ave, Fayetteville
🕐 Opening February 2027
