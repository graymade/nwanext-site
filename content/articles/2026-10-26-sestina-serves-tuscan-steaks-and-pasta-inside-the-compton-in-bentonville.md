Have you been to Sestina yet? If not, this is your sign.

Tucked inside The Compton in downtown Bentonville, Sestina is Bentonville's first Tuscan-inspired steakhouse, built around a wood-burning grill. Think 45-day dry-aged steaks, handmade pastas, and a wine list worth lingering over, all in a dining room made for a long dinner with friends. Chef Corrin Ellis runs the kitchen.

📍 Sestina, 200 E Central Ave, Bentonville · [@sestinabentonville](https://www.instagram.com/sestinabentonville/)
🗓 Dinner nightly from 5 PM (until 10 PM Sun-Thu, 11 PM Fri-Sat) · Weekend brunch Sat-Sun 10 AM-2:30 PM
🎟 Reservations recommended via OpenTable
