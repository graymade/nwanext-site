Fayetteville has a new-old place to stay ✨

The Clementine on College just opened its doors in Midtown Fayetteville. It is a reimagined 1965 mid-century hotel, the old Hi-Way Inn, with eight unique rooms, each with its own bathroom. Cafe Rue Orleans is right on the property, and Sidecar, the cocktail lounge downstairs, is steps away. Book direct at clementineoncollege.com.

📍 1156 N College Ave, Fayetteville
🛏️ 8 unique rooms, 2 king and 6 queen

[@clementine_on_college](https://www.instagram.com/clementine_on_college/)

## More details

The hotel is under new local ownership and offers 24/7 self check-in (The Clementine on College). Each room has a mini fridge, microwave and coffee maker, and guests share an outdoor seating area and parking lot (The Clementine on College). Rooms can also be booked through Airbnb and VRBO (The Clementine on College).
