Fayetteville has a new drive-thru coffee stop, and it keeps things simple in the best way. ☕

Firecat Coffee is now open on South School Avenue, pouring flavored hot brews that can be made iced with your choice of milk, all at the same price. Snickerdoodle, Pumpkin Spice, Peanut Butter Cup, and a straight-up Regular Coffee are all on the menu.

📍 1560 S School Ave, Fayetteville
🕐 Open daily, 5 AM to 5 PM
💲 Small $4, Medium $5, Large $6, and yes, they will fill your big thermos for $7

Local, drive-thru, and easy on the wallet. A very good morning stop.
