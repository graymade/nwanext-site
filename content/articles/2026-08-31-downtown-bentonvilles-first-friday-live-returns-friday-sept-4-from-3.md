Downtown Bentonville’s [@downtownbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/downtownbentonville/) First Friday Live returns Friday, Sept. 4, from 3-9 PM on and around the Bentonville Square.

The September edition brings more than 100 local vendors, food trucks, family-friendly activities and a main-stage lineup featuring [@jakobalexandermusic](https://www.instagram.com/jakobalexandermusic/), Squash Garden and [@benjamindelshreve](https://www.instagram.com/benjamindelshreve/). A complimentary Yoga Story class runs noon-1 PM before vendor booths open at 3.

Several streets around the Square will close, so organizers recommend arriving early, carpooling, biking or walking when possible.
