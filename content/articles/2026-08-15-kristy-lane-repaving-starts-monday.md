Siloam Springs says Kristy Lane will be repaved from S. Washington to E. Lake Francis starting Monday, Aug. 17, with work expected to wrap up Friday, Aug. 21.

The city says there will be no lane closures, but drivers should expect delays while paving and milling equipment is active. Daytime street parking will not be allowed during the work. The photo is representative, not Kristy Lane.
