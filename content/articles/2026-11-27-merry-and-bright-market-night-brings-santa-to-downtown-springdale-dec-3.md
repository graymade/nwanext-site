Springdale is getting a proper Christmas market night 🎄

Wander Springdale is hosting Merry and Bright Market Night on Thursday, December 3, from 5:30 to 7:30 PM at 307 W Emma Ave. Free photos with Santa (bring your phone or camera), a Christmas market full of local vendors, and optional donations that benefit Children's House in Springdale.

🎄 Free admission
📍 307 W Emma Ave, Springdale
🗓️ Thu Dec 3, 5:30 to 7:30 PM
