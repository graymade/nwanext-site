Guy Fieri came to Bentonville. 🌶️

Danger Dave’s is officially Flavortown-approved — the sandwich shop and backyard bar at 310 S Main St appeared on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives (Season 56, "Beef, Bowls, and Burritos"), which aired on Food Network Sept 11. The crew threw their own watch party out back for the premiere, projector screen and all.

It’s retro sandwich shop meets honky-tonk backyard: stacked sandwiches like The Grinder and the fried-bologna O.G., live music, and one of the biggest patios near the Square. Open Mon–Sat, closed Sundays.

## More details

Winter hours are 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Monday through Thursday and 11 a.m. to midnight Friday and Saturday, closed Sunday (Danger Dave's). The kitchen closes at 10 p.m. Monday through Thursday and at 11 p.m. Friday and Saturday (Danger Dave's).
