A new [@chickfila](https://www.instagram.com/chickfila/) restaurant is proposed for 4077 W. Wedington Drive in west Fayetteville, near Rupple Road between Catfish Hole and Walgreens.

City planning records list Chick-fil-A as the applicant for a driveway variance tied to the 1.76-acre site. Fayetteville staff recommended denying a requested second full-access driveway because of traffic-safety concerns and nearby access options. The variance remains in review, and the city records do not include an opening date.
