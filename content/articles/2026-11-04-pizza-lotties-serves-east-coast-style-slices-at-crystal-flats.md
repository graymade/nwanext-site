Bentonville, if you still haven't made it to Pizza Lottie's, consider this your nudge. 🍕

It's the sister shop to Pizzeria Ruby, doing East Coast-style pizza by the slice out at Crystal Flats. The pies are 18 inches, the slice counter runs all day, and there's more than pizza: scratch-made pastas, deli sandwiches at lunch, and beer, wine, and cocktails on tap.

That green slice on the right is the pistachio bologna pie, with mortadella, pistachio pesto, and stracciatella. The pepperoni speaks for itself.

📍 1401 NE John DeShields Blvd, Suite 135, Bentonville (Crystal Flats)
🕐 Tue–Sun, 11 AM to 9 PM (10 PM Fri & Sat) · Closed Mondays
📞 (479) 326-8399
🍦 Save room for the soft gelato and sundaes
