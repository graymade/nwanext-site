Get ready to frost, sprinkle, and snack 🎄🍪

Bentonville Brewing is hosting Frosted and Festive, a holiday cookie decorating party on tap. Each $32 kit comes with 9 holiday cookies, icing, sprinkles, and decorating supplies, plus a beer and complimentary hot chocolate. Kits can be shared, so bring the kids, grab your friends, or make it a holiday date.

📍 Bentonville Brewing, 901 SW 14th St, Bentonville
🕒 Sat Dec 5, 2 to 5 PM
🎟️ $32 per kit, grab yours through the link in [@bentonvillebrewing](https://www.instagram.com/bentonvillebrewing/) bio
