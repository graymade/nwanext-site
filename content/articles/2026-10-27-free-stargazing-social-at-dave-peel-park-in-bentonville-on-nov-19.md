A free night under the stars, right in downtown Bentonville.

Downtown Bentonville is teaming up with [@explorescientific](https://www.instagram.com/explorescientific/) for Stargazing Social on Thursday, November 19. Head to the Pavilion at Dave Peel Park from 6 to 9 PM, look through real telescopes at the moon and planets, and try hands-on sky, space, and science activities. There will be music and STEM fun too, and it is completely free.

📍 Pavilion at Dave Peel Park, 206 E Central Ave, Bentonville · [@downtownbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/downtownbentonville/) [@explorescientific](https://www.instagram.com/explorescientific/)
🗓 Thu Nov 19 · 6-9 PM · Free
