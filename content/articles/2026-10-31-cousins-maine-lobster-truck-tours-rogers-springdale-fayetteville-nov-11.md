Maine lobster is coming to Northwest Arkansas, and it is only here for five days.

The Cousins Maine Lobster truck, the Shark Tank-famous crew serving wild-caught Maine lobster, rolls through Rogers, Springdale, and Fayetteville from Wednesday, November 11 through Sunday, November 15. Every stop runs 12 to 8 PM.

The lineup is the three signature rolls, the Maine Roll served chilled with mayo, the Connecticut Roll served warm with butter and lemon, and the Garlic Butter Roll finished with garlic, Parmesan, and herbs, plus a Lobster Roll Flight if you want to try all three, along with clam chowder, lobster tacos, and whoopie pies.

📍 The tour schedule, all stops 12-8 PM:
Wed Nov 11 · Lowe's, 300 N 46th St, Rogers
Thu Nov 12 · Lowe's, 4233 W Sunset Ave, Springdale
Fri Nov 13 · Rendezvous Junction Brewing Co, 2225 S Bellview Rd, Rogers
Sat Nov 14 · New Province Brewing Co, 1310 W Hudson Rd, Rogers
Sun Nov 15 · Lowe's, 3231 W MLK Jr Blvd, Fayetteville
