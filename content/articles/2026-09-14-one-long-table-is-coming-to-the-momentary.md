One long table is coming to [@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/).

Outstanding in the Field lands in Bentonville on Saturday, Oct. 10, from 3-7:30 PM, with the end time approximate. Guest chef Micah Klasky of The Hive at 21c Museum Hotel will lead a multi-course, family-style meal following a welcome reception and, when appropriate, a property tour.

Tickets are advertised at $390. Check the organizer's checkout for the current final total.
