Fayetteville, brunch is better as a crawl. 🥂

The Holiday Haul Brunch Crawl is back on the Square for its fourth year. Your $25 ticket gets you brunch tastings and boozy and non-alcoholic drinks while you wander the local shops, all under the Lights of the Ozarks.

📍 Historic Downtown Square, Fayetteville
🗓️ Sunday, Dec 6th, 11 AM to 2 PM
🎟️ $25, tickets at downtownfay.org

Just for the adults on this one. Grab a friend and make a morning of it.
