Holiday shopping season starts this Friday in Springdale, and this is the big one. 🎁

The NWA Holiday Market takes over the NWA Convention Center for two full days of gifts, decor, clothing, jewelry, gourmet foods, and plenty of handmade finds. It is an easy way to get ahead of your list and shop local makers while you are at it.

What to know:
📍 NWA Convention Center, 1420 S 48th St, Springdale
📅 Friday, Nov 6, 9 AM to 8 PM
📅 Saturday, Nov 7, 9 AM to 6 PM

Bring a list, or just wander. You will probably leave with something you did not know you needed.
