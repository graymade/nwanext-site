Word Coffee is turning its little shop on College Avenue into a winter makers market. ☕🎄

Stop by Saturday, December 5, from 9 AM to 2 PM to browse upcycled goods, prints, and homemade treasures from seven local makers: Meme Sews, Terragem, Lit Happens Books, Lambkin Art Co, Feral Dog Prints, Hi Hands Ceramics, and Sunbear Sandwiches.

A good excuse to get out, grab a coffee, and knock out a few gifts close to home.

📍 Word Coffee, 816 N College Ave, Fayetteville
🕒 Sat Dec 5, 9 AM to 2 PM
