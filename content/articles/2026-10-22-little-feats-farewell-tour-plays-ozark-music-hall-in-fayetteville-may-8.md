🎸 One last ride. A legendary band's farewell tour stops in Fayetteville.

Little Feat — the band behind "Dixie Chicken" — brings The Last Farewell Tour to Ozark Music Hall on Sat May 8, 2027. 2027 is the band's final year of touring, and Fayetteville made the list. Doors 7 PM. Presented by Gate 52, with VIP packages available.

Tickets go on sale to the public Fri Oct 23 at 10 AM CT. The artist presale ran Wed Oct 21; the venue/newsletter presale is today (Thu Oct 22, 10 AM–10 PM CT).

📍 Ozark Music Hall — 3615 N Steele Blvd, Fayetteville · [@ozarkmusichall](https://www.instagram.com/ozarkmusichall/) [@littlefeat_official](https://www.instagram.com/littlefeat_official/)
🗓 Sat May 8, 2027 · Doors 7 PM · 🎟 on sale Fri Oct 23, 10 AM CT
