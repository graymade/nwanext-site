Six wines, six holiday bites. 🍷

baked by kori's Holiday Table tasting is back in Downtown Rogers on Wednesday, Oct 21 at 6:30 PM — a guided evening pairing six wines with six holiday-inspired bites, with ideas for what to pour around your own table this season.

The pairings run from sparkling + Parmesan-crusted artichoke heart through Pinot Noir + herb-roasted turkey and cranberry relish, closing with red sparkling + pumpkin cheesecake. Their last tasting sold out — seating is limited, 21+, advance tickets required (from $82).
