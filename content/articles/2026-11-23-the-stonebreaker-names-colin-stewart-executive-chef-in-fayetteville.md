There's a new team in the kitchen on Markham Hill.

The Stonebreaker just named Chef Colin Stewart its new executive chef, with Timothy Orway stepping in as director of food and beverage. The restaurant sits in the restored historic house at 2231 W Markham Rd, and the pair will lead its locally inspired dining room going forward.

If you have not been up the hill in a while, this is a good reason to go.

📍 The Stonebreaker, 2231 W Markham Rd, Fayetteville
🍽️ Brunch daily, happy hour 4:30 to 6 PM

[@stonebreakerhotel](https://www.instagram.com/stonebreakerhotel/)

## More details

The Restaurant at The Stonebreaker is open daily, with breakfast and lunch on weekdays, brunch from 7:30 AM to 2 PM on Saturday and Sunday, and dinner nightly from 5 to 9 PM (The Stonebreaker Hotel).
