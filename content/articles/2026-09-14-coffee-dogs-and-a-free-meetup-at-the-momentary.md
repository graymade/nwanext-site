Coffee, dogs and a free meetup at [@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/). 🐾

Leashes & Lattes with Best Friends Animal Society is happening Oct. 3 from 10-11 AM at The Momentary in Bentonville.

Meet adoptable dogs, find free pup cups for your dog and the featured dogs, and mingle with fellow dog lovers. Dogs must stay leashed.

Free, no tickets required. Event details: https://themomentary.org/calendar/community-meet-up-dog-days-with-best-friends-animal-society/
