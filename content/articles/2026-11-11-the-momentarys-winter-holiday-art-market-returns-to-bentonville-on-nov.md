Your holiday shopping can start with local makers.

The Momentary's Winter Holiday Art Market, WHAM for short, is back on Saturday, November 28. More than 20 local and regional merchants will fill RØDE House with one-of-a-kind gifts, the kind you will not find in a big box store.

It runs 10 AM to 4 PM and admission is free, no tickets needed. While you are there, you can also wander through the Chuck Ramirez photography exhibit and Lucy Sparrow's felt supermarket.

📍 The Momentary, 507 SE E St, Bentonville
🕐 Sat Nov 28, 10 AM to 4 PM
🎟️ Free, no tickets required
