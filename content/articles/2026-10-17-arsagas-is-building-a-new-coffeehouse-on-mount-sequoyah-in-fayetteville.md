☕🏔️ A new Arsaga's is headed to the mountain. Fayetteville's beloved coffeehouse is opening a new café on Mount Sequoyah — the usual Arsaga's coffeehouse, plus what they're calling a great gathering spot on the mountain. Co-owner Cary Arsaga toured the build-out this week; no opening date yet, but we'll be first in line when those doors open. via [@flavorsofnorthwestarkansas](https://www.instagram.com/flavorsofnorthwestarkansas/)
