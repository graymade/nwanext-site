Bentonville's sushi scene is about to level up.

Yokozuna, the Tulsa favorite known for hand-rolled sushi since 2009, is opening its first Northwest Arkansas location on 8th Street at the Walmart Home Office campus, near Sweetgreen and Anthropologie. The restaurant's own site has the Bentonville spot listed as opening soon, with the address and phone number already live.

The name is the highest rank in sumo, and the menu plays big: signature rolls, ramen, wok-fired plates, and a full bar pouring cocktails and sake, with a daily happy hour. No opening date has been announced yet, but the buildout looks finished, so keep an eye on this one.

## More details

Yokozuna's site now lists the Bentonville location as open daily, Monday to Thursday and Sunday 11 AM to 9 PM, Friday and Saturday 11 AM to 10 PM (Yokozuna). The phone number is (479) 579-9299 (Yokozuna).
