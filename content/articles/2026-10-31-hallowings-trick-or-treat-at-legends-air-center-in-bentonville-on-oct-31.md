Today is the day to trick-or-treat somewhere a little different.

HalloWings lands at Legends Air Center in Bentonville this afternoon, and it is free for the whole family. Kids in costume can trick-or-treat around decorated airplanes inside the Exhibit Hangar, snap photos with vintage and modern planes, and grab something sweet from the Do It Yum coffee and ice cream truck and The Cozy Crumb sweets cart. Next door on Lake Bentonville, there is an RC sailboat regatta to watch too.

📍 Legends Air Center, 2205 SW I St, Bentonville · [@flyoz](https://www.instagram.com/flyoz/)
🕐 Today, Sat Oct 31, 1-4 PM · Free, family-friendly
