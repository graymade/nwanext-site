Fayetteville, your pasta nights are about to get a whole lot easier. 🍝

[@zellipasta](https://www.instagram.com/zellipasta/) is opening its second Northwest Arkansas location near Gulley Park, in the former Township Baptist Church building in East Fayetteville. The Springdale pasta shop is the first commercial tenant announced for the 1.17-acre redevelopment, which also adds neighborhood shops, offices, and eight townhomes.

What to know:
📍 Near Gulley Park, East Fayetteville
🥪 Ground floor sandwich shop and bodega serving sandwiches and pasta
🇮🇹 Plus an Italian market, and possibly homemade ice cream
🏗️ Construction on the full redevelopment wraps in June 2027, with townhomes on sale in early 2027

Zelli makes its bronze-cut pasta in-house in Springdale, and the new spot brings that same shop to Fayetteville's east side. No opening date for the shop itself yet, but this one is worth keeping on your radar.
