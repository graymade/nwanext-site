Calling all wieners 🐾

Good Dog Cafe is throwing a Dachshund Pawty today, and every dachshund (and every mix of the breed) is invited. The park closes to the public for this one, so it is just the long dogs, out to romp and meet the other wieners of Fayetteville. A limited food and drink menu runs the whole event.

Admission is $12 per dog, and a waiver gets signed before you head into the park.

📍 Good Dog Cafe, 2640 E Huntsville Rd, Fayetteville
🕒 Today, Sat Nov 21, 3 to 4:30 PM
🎟️ $12 per dog
