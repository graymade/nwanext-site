Rogers, the best coffee shop in the world is right here in our backyard.

Onyx Coffee Lab took the top spot on the World's 100 Best Coffee Shops list for 2026, the first American shop ever to do it. More than 15,000 shops were nominated around the world, and the final ranking came from 800 coffee experts plus a public vote. Onyx finished second last year, and this year they took the whole thing.

It started in 2012 as a tiny cafe in Springdale. The flagship is still in downtown Rogers, in the historic 1907 building, and it is worth the trip.

📍 101 E Walnut Ave, Rogers
