Downtown Bentonville’s [@downtownbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/downtownbentonville/) Movie Nights on the Square returns Thursday, Sept. 10, with a free outdoor screening of Ratatouille.

The event runs 5:30–8 PM on the Bentonville Square, with the movie starting at 6 PM. Bring lawn chairs, picnic blankets and your favorite theater snacks. Admission is free, and organizers describe the event as family-friendly. Plan to arrive early enough to find a spot before the screen comes on.
