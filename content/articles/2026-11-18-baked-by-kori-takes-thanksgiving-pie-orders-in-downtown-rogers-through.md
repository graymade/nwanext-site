Thanksgiving pie, handled. 🥧

Baked by Kori in downtown Rogers is taking pre-orders for Thanksgiving pies now through Sunday, Nov 22. Pick your fighter: pumpkin, apple, or apple cran crisp. Each one is $45 and comes in a glass pie pan that is yours to keep.

Order at bakedbykori.com/thanksgiving-pies, then pick up in downtown Rogers on Tuesday, Nov 24 from 11 AM to 7 PM or Wednesday, Nov 25 from 9 AM to 1 PM. Pickup is at the downtown Rogers shop only.
