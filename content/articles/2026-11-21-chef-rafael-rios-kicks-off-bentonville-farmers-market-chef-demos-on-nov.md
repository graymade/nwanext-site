Chef demos are back at the Bentonville Farmers Market 👨‍🍳

The indoor market is bringing back its chef demo series, and the kickoff could not be better. Five-time James Beard nominee Chef Rafael Rios of [@yeyosnwa](https://www.instagram.com/yeyosnwa/) is cooking live this Saturday, showing off recipes made with ingredients from the market stalls.

Come watch, learn a few tricks, and stock up on market favorites while you are there.

📍 Record, 104 SW A St, Bentonville
🗓️ Saturday, Nov 21st, 10 AM — indoor market runs 9 AM to 12 PM
