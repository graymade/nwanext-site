Fayetteville, the Garden is glowing again ✨

WinterFest is back at the Botanical Garden of the Ozarks, Tuesdays and Saturdays from November 28 through December 19. Four Saturdays of holiday magic: story time with Santa on Nov 28, a local Winter Market on Dec 5, a Pawliday Party with adoptable pets on Dec 12, and a Family Glow Party under the stars on Dec 19. Every date brings food trucks and glowing Garden lights, and Tuesday evenings are free.

🎄 Four Saturdays: Nov 28, Dec 5, 12, and 19
💡 Light Stroll 5 to 7 PM on Nov 28, Dec 5, and Dec 12
🎟️ $14 adults, $7 ages 4 to 17, free 3 and under
📍 4703 N Crossover Rd, Fayetteville

Come during the day and your ticket gets you back in for the evening Light Stroll. Registration is required for everyone, including members, so grab your spot ahead of time. Season passes are $75 and cover four guests for all four Saturdays.

[@bgozarks](https://www.instagram.com/bgozarks/)

## More details

Tuesday sessions are free as an extension of grant funding from Terrific Tuesday Nights, while Saturday programs are ticketed (Botanical Garden of the Ozarks).
