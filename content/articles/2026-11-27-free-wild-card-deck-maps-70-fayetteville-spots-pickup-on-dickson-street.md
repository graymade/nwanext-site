Fayetteville, your weekend plans just got dealt for you 🎴

Wild Card is a free deck of 70 collectible cards, each one a locally curated Fayetteville spot, from Hammontree's Grilled Cheese to Prairie Street Bar and Tap. Take the quick quiz at staywildcard.com, get your card numbers, then pick up your mystery cards at the Pretty Good Neighbors checkout desk in the Puritan breezeway on Dickson. The newest card in the deck is Lights of the Ozarks, right on time for the season.

🎴 Free, 70 local places
📍 Pretty Good Neighbors, 205 W Dickson St, Fayetteville
🕚 Tue to Sat 11 AM to 6 PM, Sun 12 to 4 PM
