Big news for a little ice cream shop 🏆

Fayetteville's Crème Ice Cream Co. just brought home a national Blue Ribbon. Their Ugandan Vanilla earned the honor from the North American Ice Cream Association at ConeCon 2026 in Palm Springs, and it even scored a perfect mark in microbiological testing. Not bad for a family-run shop on College Ave.

📍 Crème Ice Cream Co., 2730 N College Ave, Fayetteville
