Effin — the Nashville producer behind the new Heartbeats album — is bringing his first-ever full-scale headline tour to Ozark Music Hall. The 37-date Heartbeats Tour lands in Fayetteville on Wed Mar 10, 2027, with support from Nikita, the Wicked + AYCH.

🎟 Tickets go on sale to the public TODAY (Fri Oct 23) at 10 AM CT. Presented by Gate 52. Doors 7 PM.

📍 Ozark Music Hall — 3615 N Steele Blvd, Fayetteville · [@ozarkmusichall](https://www.instagram.com/ozarkmusichall/) [@effinofficial](https://www.instagram.com/effinofficial/) [@aychofficial](https://www.instagram.com/aychofficial/)
🗓 Wed Mar 10, 2027 · Doors 7 PM · 🎟 on sale today 10 AM CT
