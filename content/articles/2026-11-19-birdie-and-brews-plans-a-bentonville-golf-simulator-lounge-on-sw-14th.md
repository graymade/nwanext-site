Bentonville, your golf game is about to get an indoor home. ⛳

Birdie and Brews is bringing its simulator lounge and taproom to Bentonville in 2027, upstairs from Bentonville Brewing Co. Expect six simulator bays, 36 self-serve taps, and games like pool and Krazy Darts.

📍 901 SW 14th St, Bentonville (above Bentonville Brewing Co.)
🗓️ Opening in 2027

The Rogers spot has been going since December 2024, so Bentonville is getting the second location from local owners Tyler and Shania Watkins.

## More details

The Rogers location is at 2223 S Bellview Rd, Suite 101, and uses Full Swing simulators (Birdie and Brews).
