🍕 College Ave is getting a new pizza joint.

Thai Dinner — the longtime staple at 514 N College Ave — has closed its doors, and Johnny Brusco's New York Style Pizza is taking over the building. It'll be the chain's second NWA location, with an opening targeted for early next year. Expect NY-style pies, calzones, subs, and wings.

📍 514 N College Ave, Fayetteville
🗓 Opening early 2027
