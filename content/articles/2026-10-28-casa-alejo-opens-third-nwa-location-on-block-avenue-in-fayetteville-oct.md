Fayetteville, your new favorite Mexican spot is about to be a whole lot closer.

Casa Alejo opens its third Northwest Arkansas location this Thursday, October 29, at 102 N Block Ave, in the former Tables and Ale building just steps from the Square and Dickson Street. The family-run favorite already has beloved spots in downtown Springdale and downtown Rogers, and this one brings the same menu staples with it: slow-cooked beef birria, ribeye fajitas, cheese dip, and plenty of poblano sauce.
