🍪 Gooey-center cookie people, your second Arkansas location has arrived.

Half Baked Goodness is now open in Fayetteville at 2518 N College Ave, Suite 136 — the cookie chain's second Arkansas store (the other is in Rogers). Per the brand's own post on Oct 10: "Rogers & Fayetteville are ready to serve you!"

Their whole thing: "Half Baked is our name. Gooey is our thing." Giant cookies, fully baked but intentionally soft in the center, with rotating weekly "Cookies of the Week," cookie cakes, plus keto, gluten-free, and vegan options. Online ordering, delivery, and pickup available. Their words to live by: "Life is Short…Make it Sweet!"
