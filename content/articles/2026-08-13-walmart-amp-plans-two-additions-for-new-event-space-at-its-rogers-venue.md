Walmart AMP is proposing a two-story front-of-house addition and a north addition at its Rogers venue.

City records show commercial plan review `CPR26-0126` at 5079 W. Northgate Road. Plans reported by Talk Business & Politics call for a 9,432-square-foot front-of-house addition and a 9,153-square-foot north addition.

Walton Arts Center described the project as new event space but has not released additional details.

## More details

The north addition includes 22 accessible parking spaces (Talk Business & Politics). The project has not been approved; city staff said it can be approved administratively if it meets code, or go to the Board of Adjustment if a variance is needed (Talk Business & Politics).
