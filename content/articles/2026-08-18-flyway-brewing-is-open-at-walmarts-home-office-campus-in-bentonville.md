Craft brewery and kitchen [@flywaybentonville](https://www.instagram.com/flywaybentonville/) is now open at Walmart's Home Office campus in Bentonville.

The new location is at 1410 SE 8th Street, Suite 1. Flyway lists service Monday through Wednesday from 3 to 9 p.m., Thursday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., and Friday and Saturday from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Representative craft beer photo, not the Bentonville location.
