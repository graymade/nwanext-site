[@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/) brings the Aux Arc Record Fair to RØDE House on Sunday, Sept. 20, from 12-5 PM.

Browse crates of vinyl, vintage clothing and vintage audio gear, with local vendors, food trucks and plenty of records to discover. The Momentary lists tickets at $5, or $4 for members. Reserve online or through the box office.

📍 RØDE House at the Momentary, Bentonville
