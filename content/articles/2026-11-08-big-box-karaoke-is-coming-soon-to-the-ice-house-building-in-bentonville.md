Bentonville, warm up those vocal cords. 🎤

Big Box Karaoke is almost ready for you. The Fayetteville favorite is putting the finishing touches on its second location in the Ice House Building, and the sign is already up.

If you haven't been, it's private suite karaoke: you rent your own room, sing with your people instead of a whole bar, and food and drinks from the full menu come right to your suite. Seven private rooms, all ages welcome, built for birthdays, team nights, and girls' nights alike.

The original has been a Block Avenue staple since New Year's Eve 2018. No opening date has been announced yet for Bentonville, but by the looks of it, it won't be long.
