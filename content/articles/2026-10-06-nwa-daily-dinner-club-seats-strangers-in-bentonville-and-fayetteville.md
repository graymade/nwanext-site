Dinner with strangers. Leave as friends. 🍽️

NWA Daily's Dinner Club is back on Thursday, Oct 22 — and it's the easiest yes of the month. Comment DINNER on [@nwa.daily](https://www.instagram.com/nwa.daily/)'s post, take a quick personality quiz, and get matched with five fellow NWA-ers for dinner at a mystery restaurant in Bentonville or Fayetteville (revealed the morning of). After dinner, all the groups meet up at a nearby bar to keep the night going. Booking is $15.99 — you just cover your own meal.

📍 Bentonville + Fayetteville (restaurant revealed day-of)
🗓 Thu Oct 22 · 6:30 PM
💬 Comment DINNER on [@nwa.daily](https://www.instagram.com/nwa.daily/)'s post to sign up
