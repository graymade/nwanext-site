An autumn night at the Garden. 🍂

The Botanical Garden of the Ozarks' Autumn Gala at the Garden returns Friday, Oct. 23, 2026, from 6-9 PM in Fayetteville. This year's Through the Torii Gate theme celebrates Japanese design, with a seated dinner, entertainment, the evening program, silent auction and wine pull.

Tickets are $135 for one or $250 for two. Get event details and tickets from [@bgozarks](https://www.instagram.com/bgozarks/).
