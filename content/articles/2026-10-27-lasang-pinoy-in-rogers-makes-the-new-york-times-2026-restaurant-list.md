A sizzling plate of sisig in Rogers just picked up about the biggest cosign a restaurant can get.

Lasang Pinoy, the family-run Filipino spot on W. Walnut, made the New York Times' 2026 Restaurant List, the paper's 50 favorite restaurants in America right now. It is the only Arkansas restaurant on the list.

The place is run by siblings Maria Smith and Bill Martfeld, who leads the kitchen, and everything is built on family recipes: sisig on a sizzling plate, lumpia, chicken adobo, pancit. Bring a crew and you can book a boodle fight or a karaoke room. It is also the first brick-and-mortar Filipino restaurant in Northwest Arkansas, and it still feels like a hidden gem.

📍 Lasang Pinoy, 719 W Walnut St Ste 104, Rogers · [@lasangpinoynwa](https://www.instagram.com/lasangpinoynwa/)
