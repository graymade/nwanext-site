Free, all-ages fun is moving to Saturday: [@uarkbumpers](https://www.instagram.com/uarkbumpers/) will host the Arkansas Insect Festival on Sept. 12 in Fayetteville.

The festival runs 9 AM-3 PM at the Pauline Whitaker Animal Science Center, 1335 W. Knapp Drive. A quieter sensory-friendly hour is scheduled for 9-10 AM. Visitors can meet entomologists, see live arthropods and planned exhibits, and cheer on Madagascar hissing cockroaches in the returning roach races. Admission is free, and all ages are welcome.
