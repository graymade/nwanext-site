Pajamas on, all aboard. 🚂

The Arkansas and Missouri Railroad's Holiday Express Pajama Train rolls out of the Springdale Depot starting Saturday, November 28. It is a one-hour round trip to Johnson in vintage cars, with hot cocoa, cookies, Christmas stories, carols, and Santa visiting for photos. Saturday departures are 11 AM, 1 PM, and 3 PM, and the 8 PM ride is already on a waitlist, so book ahead. The train runs select dates through December 19.

🚂 Season starts Sat Nov 28
📍 Springdale Depot, downtown Springdale
🎟️ Coach $25 adults, $17.50 kids 3 to 12, $2 lap toddlers under 3

## More details

The railroad offers group discounts for parties of 12 or more, booked through its event request form or by calling 479-725-4017 (Arkansas & Missouri Railroad).
