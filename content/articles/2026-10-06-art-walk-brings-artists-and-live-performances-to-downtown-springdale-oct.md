Springdale is turning its streets into a gallery. 🎨

Tickets are $20 and include a sample drink at each participating stop (non-alcoholic options available). Early-bird deal: code ARTWALK26 takes 25% off — just $15 — through Oct 10. Presented by the Tyson Family Foundation.

📍 Downtown Springdale · check-in at The Apollo on Emma, 308 W Emma Ave
🗓 Thu Oct 15 · 5:30–8:30 PM

[@downtownspringdale](https://www.instagram.com/downtownspringdale/)

## More details

Ticket holders get the full event map, with live performance stages, more than 20 participating businesses and more than 100 featured artists (Downtown Springdale Alliance). All ticket purchases are final and non-refundable (Downtown Springdale Alliance).
