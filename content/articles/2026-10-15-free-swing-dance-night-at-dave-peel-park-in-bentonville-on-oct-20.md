💃 One last dance, Bentonville! Downtown Bentonville's Downtown Dance series wraps 2026 with swing night: Tue Oct 20 at the Pavilion at Dave Peel Park. Instructor-led class kicks off at 6 PM, then social dancing till 8 PM, led by [@nwaswingdancesociety](https://www.instagram.com/nwaswingdancesociety/). No partner needed, no experience needed — and it's FREE. Go learn something new before the season ends. 🕺

📍 Pavilion at Dave Peel Park, Bentonville
