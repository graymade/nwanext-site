Dust off the record you always reach for, because this night the playlist is yours.

Walton Arts Center's BYO Vinyl Night is back on Thursday, November 5 with Retro Rewind. Bring your favorite records to the Alexander Gallery and let the DJs from [@odeoncollective](https://www.instagram.com/odeoncollective/) spin your picks. Think classic rock, Motown, soul, and all your favorite old-school sounds, surrounded by the Our Art, Our Region, Our Time exhibition.

📍 Alexander Gallery at Walton Arts Center, Fayetteville · [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/)
🗓 Thu Nov 5 · 6-8 PM · Free and open to the public
🍷 Grab a drink or a bite from a nearby spot on your way in

Want to keep the '70s going? The Carpenters Songbook: A Live Celebration is at 7 PM that same evening in Baum Walker Hall. Concert tickets are sold separately.

## More details

The Alexander Gallery is at Porter Art Warehouse, 212 N West Ave, Fayetteville, and no reservation is needed (Walton Arts Center). The next BYO Vinyl Night after this one is Thursday, Dec. 3, from 6 to 8 PM (Walton Arts Center).
