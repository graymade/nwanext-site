🎻 A GRAMMY-winning string trio. Ten-dollar tickets. Fayetteville, you're spoiled.

Time for Three — the GRAMMY and Emmy-winning trio of violinists Charles Yang and Nicolas Kendall and double bassist Ranaan Meyer — returns to Walton Arts Center on Tue Nov 10 at 7 PM. They call themselves a "classically trained garage band": classical, Americana and singer-songwriter bent into one high-energy show. As part of WAC's 10x10 Arts Series, tickets are just $10 — and 15–20 Rogers High School students join them on stage.

📍 Walton Arts Center, Baum Walker Hall — 495 W Dickson St, Fayetteville · [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/) [@timeforthree](https://www.instagram.com/timeforthree/)
🗓 Tue Nov 10 · 7 PM · $10
