Fayetteville says construction on a 220-foot sidewalk gap along the north side of Mission Boulevard will begin Monday, Aug. 17. The work is between Libby Lane and Winwood Drive and is scheduled through Oct. 1.

The westbound lane will close periodically from 9 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Both lanes will remain open during school drop-off and pickup times. The photo is representative, not the Fayetteville project site.
