The holidays start on the mountain this Saturday.

Mount Sequoyah kicks off its Festival of Trees series with a Jazz Brunch on Saturday, November 14, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM in the Bailey Center. Brunch from Early Bird Catering, live jazz from The Cocktail Club Trio, all surrounded by the decorated trees. The series runs through December 11, with a trail run, trivia night, winter market, and a Merry Mingle finale still to come.

Tickets for the brunch start at $135 and include brunch, jazz, drinks, and festival admission. It is 21 and up, and every ticket helps fund improvements to the Bailey Center.

📍 Mount Sequoyah, 150 N Skyline Dr, Fayetteville
🕐 Sat Nov 14, 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM
🎟️ Tickets from $135, 21 and up
