💙💛 Walk with purpose this Saturday.

The Down Syndrome Connection of Northwest Arkansas hosts its annual Step Up for Down Syndrome Walk — Sat Oct 24, 1–4 PM at The Jones Center in Springdale. Expect a DJ, dancing, karaoke, snacks, inflatables, gymnastics, face painting and more — all fueling DSCNWA's support, connection, and learning programs for individuals with Down syndrome and their families across the region.

🎟 $10 general admission (ages 3+); kids 2 and under + DSCNWA members free. Tickets at dscnwa.org.

📍 The Jones Center — 922 E Emma Ave, Springdale · [@dscnwa](https://www.instagram.com/dscnwa/) [@thejonescenter](https://www.instagram.com/thejonescenter/)
🗓 Sat Oct 24 · 1–4 PM
