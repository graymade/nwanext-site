There is a new herd in south Fayetteville. 🦬

Local artist Caite Mae Ramos recently finished a black-and-white bison mural on the east-facing wall at South Yard, near the new HomeGrown restaurant. The bison honor the prairies that once stretched across Northwest Arkansas, and the site's past as a farmers co-op and a railroad junction.

Ramos is an Oglala Lakota citizen who grew up in NWA, and she was South Yard's first artist in residence.

You can see it in the South Yard parking lot, at School and MLK.
