[@amazeum](https://www.instagram.com/amazeum/) opens The Hangout on Saturday, Nov. 7.

Bentonville is getting a new public gathering spot: about 34,740 square feet of new indoor and outdoor space with the Headwaters Café & Lounge (Onyx coffee and lunch), shaded seating, and room for Greenway users and neighbors. The new spaces are open with no museum admission required.

📍 Scott Family Amazeum, Bentonville
🗓 Saturday, Nov. 7
🎟 No admission required for The Hangout, as listed by the Amazeum.

https://www.amazeum.org/blog

## More details

The Scott Family Amazeum is at 1009 Museum Way in Bentonville (Scott Family Amazeum).
