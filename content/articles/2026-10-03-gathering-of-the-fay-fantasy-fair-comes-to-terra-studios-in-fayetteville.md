The fairies are gathering in Fayetteville. 🧚

Gathering of the Fay’s fall fantasy fair — "Sapphire Wings Among the Falling Leaves" — lands at Terra Studios on Saturday, Oct 10, 10am–4pm: fae vendors with crafts, treasures and treats, live music, face painting, the Wizard’s Quest, games, and the Gathering of Glamour costume contest (sign up by 1:30pm).

Admission is free. (Terra Studios also runs a separate $35 Fairy Door class at 11am & 2pm.)

📍 12103 Hazel Valley Rd, Fayetteville
🗓 Sat Oct 10 · 10am–4pm · Free
