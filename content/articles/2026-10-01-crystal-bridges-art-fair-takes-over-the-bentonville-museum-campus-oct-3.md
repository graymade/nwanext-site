Two days of art, food, and fall weather — free.

[@crystalbridgesmuseum](https://www.instagram.com/crystalbridgesmuseum/)'s Art Fair is back this weekend: Saturday 10am–6pm and Sunday 10am–4pm, campus-wide with artists from across the country, food trucks, live art demos, and culinary experiences. Free, no tickets needed. Early birds can grab the First Look preview Saturday 8:30–10am ($20, $18 members) with a mimosa in hand.

Cap Sunday with the season finale of Sunset Sundays & Art Market — 5–8pm at the A St Promenade, with jazz 6–8pm at Dave Peel Park Pavilion (via [@downtownbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/downtownbentonville/)).

📍 Crystal Bridges, 600 Museum Way, Bentonville
🗓 Sat Oct 3 (10–6) & Sun Oct 4 (10–4)
