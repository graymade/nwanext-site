Raising Cane’s is proposing a new north Fayetteville location south of 3365 N. College Ave.

Plans call for a 2,899-square-foot restaurant with a drive-thru. The Fayetteville Planning Commission is scheduled to review the proposal on September 14 at 5:30 p.m.

If approved, the company hopes to begin construction in early 2027 and open that fall.
