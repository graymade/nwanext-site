The national championships are coming back to Fayetteville, and the racing is as close as it gets.

USA Cycling's Cyclocross National Championships return to Centennial Park Dec 9 through 13, for the second year in a row on the same mountaintop course where the world championships ran in 2022. Elite, collegiate, and amateur riders race for national titles, with the elite women and men deciding theirs Saturday afternoon, Dec 12.

Cyclocross is built for watching: short laps, steep climbs, sand pits, and stairs riders have to carry their bikes up. Costumes, bells, and cowbells encouraged. Spectator admission is free, with food trucks on site.

📍 Centennial Park, Fayetteville
🗓️ Wed Dec 9 through Sun Dec 13
🎟️ Free to watch, details at cxnats.usacycling.org

## More details

The 2026 championships are presented by The Meteor and run on four course layouts at Centennial Park (USA Cycling). The Elite, U23 and Junior 17-18 races stream live on FloBikes (USA Cycling).
