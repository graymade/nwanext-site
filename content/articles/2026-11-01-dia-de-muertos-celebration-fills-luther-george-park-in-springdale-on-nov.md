Springdale, one of the most beautiful afternoons of the year is next Saturday.

Casa de la Cultura Mexicana's Día de Muertos celebration returns for its 12th year at Luther George Park. It is a free, family-friendly afternoon honoring loved ones who have passed, through music, art, community, and remembrance.

Expect folklórico dance performances, live Mexican and Latino music, local food and artisan vendors, family-friendly activities, a community ofrenda, and a Catrina contest. Whether you are celebrating your own traditions or learning about them for the first time, everyone is welcome.

🕐 Sat Nov 7, 12-6 PM · Free
