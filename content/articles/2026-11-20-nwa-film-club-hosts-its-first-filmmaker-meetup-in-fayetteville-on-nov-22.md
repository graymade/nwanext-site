Calling all local filmmakers. 🎬

NWA Film Club is hosting its first-ever filmmaker meetup this Sunday, November 22, from 2 to 4 PM at Punk Truck Stop in Fayetteville. Grab a coffee inside, talk shop with other film folks, and listen to music from your favorite films. They will even have conversation cards to break the ice.
