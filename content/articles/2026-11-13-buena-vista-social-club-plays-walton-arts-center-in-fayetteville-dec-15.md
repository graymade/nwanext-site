Havana is coming to Dickson Street. 🎶

Buena Vista Social Club, the Tony and Grammy award-winning Broadway musical, takes over Baum Walker Hall for eight performances December 15 through 20. It tells the story of legendary singer Omara Portuondo and the making of the iconic album, with a ten-piece band live on stage, a score sung entirely in Spanish and a story told in English.

The show won four Tony Awards plus a first-of-its-kind Tony honoring the whole onstage band, and the original cast album took home the Grammy for best musical theater album. Recommended for ages 8 and up.

📍 Walton Arts Center, 495 W Dickson St, Fayetteville
🗓️ Tue Dec 15 to Sun Dec 20, eight performances
🎟️ Tickets from $42 at waltonartscenter.org
