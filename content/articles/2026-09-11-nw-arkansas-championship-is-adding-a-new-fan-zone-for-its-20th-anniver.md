[@nwachampionship](https://www.instagram.com/nwachampionship/) is adding a new Fan Zone for its 20th anniversary weekend in Rogers.

From Sept. 25-27, fans can find interactive activities, food and drinks, the Paws 'N Rec Puppy Putt Challenge, and a Fan Shop with tournament and commemorative gear. The Fan Shop runs 9 AM-5 PM Friday-Sunday.

Competition is Sept. 25-27 at Pinnacle Country Club. Follow [@nwachampionship](https://www.instagram.com/nwachampionship/) for tournament details.
