Bentonville is getting a brand-new holiday market. 🎄

The first-ever Downtown Holiday Market pops up Friday, December 4, from 5 to 10 PM on A Street Promenade. Expect seasonal finds and gifts from local vendors, right in the middle of the downtown lights.

A good excuse to get your shopping done close to home.

## More details

Downtown Bentonville Inc. says the evening is modeled on European holiday markets and will turn the entire A Street Promenade into a winter wonderland of local makers and small businesses (Downtown Bentonville Inc.).
