[@thefreshmonkee](https://www.instagram.com/thefreshmonkee/) is now open in Fayetteville — its first Arkansas location.

The protein shake shop at 3761 N Mall Ave blends handcrafted shakes with real fruit, natural nut butters, and high-quality whey protein — no added sugar. They soft-opened in late August, and the Fayetteville Chamber's official ribbon cutting is Wednesday, Sept. 30 at 2 PM.

📍 3761 N Mall Ave, Fayetteville
🗓 Open now · Ribbon cutting Sept. 30, 2 PM

## More details

Hours are 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Saturday and Sunday (Fresh Monkee). The shop's phone number is (479) 935-3153 (Fresh Monkee).
