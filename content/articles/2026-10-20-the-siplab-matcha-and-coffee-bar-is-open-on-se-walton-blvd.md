🍵 Matcha people, this one's for you.

The SipLab — Bentonville's matcha + coffee bar — is open at 1501 SE Walton Blvd, Suite 103. It's a matcha-forward beverage studio: strawberry matcha, ube matcha, fig matcha, coconut cloud (matcha over coconut water), banana matcha — plus coffee drinks like the ube latte, fig latte, cocoa puff, and cinnamon toast, and croffles including the Biscoff croffle.

Closed Mondays. Check their page for current hours.
