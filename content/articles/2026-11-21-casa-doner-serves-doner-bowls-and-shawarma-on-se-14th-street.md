Bentonville has a Mediterranean spot worth knowing about 🧡

Casa Doner is the cozy little bite shop on SE 14th, right next door to Vivace. Build your own doner bowls loaded with fresh toppings and sauces, shawarma wraps, and a falafel pizza that regulars keep raving about. Save room for the house-made baklava.

📍 1100 SE 14th St, Suite 10, Bentonville
🕚 Mon to Sat, 11 AM to 8:30 PM
