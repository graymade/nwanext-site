One year of steam in the woods 🎉

South Woods Sauna and Guesthouse is turning one, and they are celebrating the best way they know how: a whole weekend of wood-fired heat, cold contrast, and community out near Lake Sequoyah.

Here is the lineup:

SATURDAY, NOV 21
🧖 Saturday Sauna Club, 11 AM to 4:30 PM
New 60-minute sessions all day, with towels, cool water, and anniversary snacks. Multi-passes welcome.

🌙 The Woods After Hours, 6 to 9 PM
Sauna cycles with ceremonial cacao, vinyl spinning indoors, and tea and towel service included. Alcohol-free and kept small on purpose, so book ahead. This one needs its own ticket, and multi-passes do not apply.

SUNDAY, NOV 22
🌲 A Full Day of Tended Sauna, 11 AM to 8:30 PM
90-minute social and silent sessions with wood-fired heat, cold contrast, tea service, and aromatherapy. Multi-passes welcome.

All weekend there are post-sauna snacks and drinks, new merch, and an anniversary raffle for a five-pass sauna package.

📍 South Woods Sauna and Guesthouse, 17097 Lake Sequoyah Dr, Fayetteville
🎟️ Single passes $55, book at southwoodsnwa.com
