Fayetteville, the lights are back on.

The Stewart Family Christmas Lights open tonight in east Fayetteville, a walk-through display with over 7 million lights and hundreds of inflatables, plus Santa every night and concessions along the way. It started in one family's yard back in 2003, and it has been a Fayetteville holiday tradition ever since.

📍 4279 E Wyman Rd, Fayetteville
🗓️ Opening night tonight, Sat Nov 14, 6 to 11 PM
🎟️ $10 admission
Open nightly through Jan 2. Sunday to Thursday 5 to 10 PM, Friday and Saturday 4:30 to 11 PM.
