👻🎃 Springdale's biggest Halloween night is back.

Springdale Parks & Recreation and the Springdale Police Department present the Halloween Fest — in the organizers' own words, "Northwest Arkansas' biggest and best community Halloween event." Saturday, Oct 24, 4–7 PM at Luther George Park, and it's all FREE: candy giveaways, carnival games, vendor booths, a PD touch-a-truck, and the one-of-a-kind Fire Department pumpkin drop to close the night. The costume contest starts at 4:30 (Little Pumpkins 0–5, Ghouls & Goblins 6–12, Teen Fright Night 13–18, Frightfully Grown 19+, plus a Monster Mash group category — pre-registration is free). Stick around after: a free Hocus Pocus screening on the park stage at ~7:15.

📍 Luther George Park, 300 Park St, Springdale
🗓 Sat Oct 24 · 4–7 PM · FREE
