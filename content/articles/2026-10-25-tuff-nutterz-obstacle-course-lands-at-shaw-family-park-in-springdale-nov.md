The world's favorite inflatable obstacle course is bouncing into Springdale.

Tuff Nutterz lands at Shaw Family Park on Saturday and Sunday, November 7 and 8, with a 1,000-foot course, a 20-foot slide, food trucks and music. Sessions run two hours, with start times at 9 AM, 11 AM, 1 PM and 3 PM, and capacity is limited per session, so booking ahead is the move.

📍 Shaw Family Park, Springdale · [@tuffnutterz.us](https://www.instagram.com/tuffnutterz.us/)
🗓 Sat-Sun Nov 7-8 · Sessions 9 AM, 11 AM, 1 PM, 3 PM
🎟 Tickets from $22, family packs $79.99 at tuffnutterz.us
