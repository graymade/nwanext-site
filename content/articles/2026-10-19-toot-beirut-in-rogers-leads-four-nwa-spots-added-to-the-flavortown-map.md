🍔🔥 Flavortown just got four new Northwest Arkansas stops.

Guy Fieri filmed Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives at four local restaurants — both episodes aired back in August — and FlavortownUSA's August roundup just added all four to the official Flavortown map. Leading the charge: Toot Beirut in Rogers, where Chef Khaled's authentic shawarma earned what the roundup calls "the fist-bump of approval from Guy himself" (kebob halabi + meat shawarma made the cut). Rounding out the map: Mezzaluna Pasteria and Blu Fish House in Bentonville, and Saiwok Vietnamese street food in Rogers.
