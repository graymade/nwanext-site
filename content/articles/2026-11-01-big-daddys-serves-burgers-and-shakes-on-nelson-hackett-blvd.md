Have you been to Big Daddy's yet?

This Fayetteville burger spot opened in May in a former service station, and it is the kind of place that keeps it simple: bold burgers, fresh-cut fries, and shakes made with Hugs Ice Cream, the sister brand that makes its ice cream right here in Fayetteville.

Start with The Big Daddy, the B.A.P., El Camino, or The Cosmic, then finish with a hand-spun shake or a scoop of Hugs. It is locally owned, part of the Golden Table Collective family, and an easy pick for a family dinner or a post-game meal.
