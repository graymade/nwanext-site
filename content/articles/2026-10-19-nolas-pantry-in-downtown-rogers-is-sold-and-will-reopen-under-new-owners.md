🛒 Downtown Rogers' foodie playground has new owners — and a reopening on the way.

Nola's Pantry — the specialty food market + café at 103 W Chestnut St that calls itself "NWA's Foodie Playground" (named for founder Bill Rumsey's mother, Nola) — has been sold and will reopen under new ownership. First spotted by [@wesellrestaurants_nwa](https://www.instagram.com/wesellrestaurants_nwa/): the shop "will be back in action soon," with full opening details to come on Nola's own page. No reopening date and no new-owner names have been published yet. When it returns: bulk spices, rubs, hot sauces, jams and gifts on the retail side, breakfast + lunch from the café side.

📍 103 W Chestnut St Ste 100, Rogers
🗓 Reopening date TBD — watch [@nolaspantry_dtr](https://www.instagram.com/nolaspantry_dtr/) for details
