A coffee kiosk with a vineyard view — yes, really.

Creekside Coffee is now open on the grounds of [@sassafrasspringsvineyard](https://www.instagram.com/sassafrasspringsvineyard/) in Springdale: an outdoor coffee stand with deck seating, red umbrellas, and wine barrels among the trees. Catch it mornings only — Thursday through Saturday, 7–11am. (Evenings, it's very much still a winery.)

📍 6461 E Guy Terry Rd, Springdale (Sassafras Springs Vineyard)
🗓 Thu–Sat, 7–11am

## More details

The winery is open Tuesday and Wednesday 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., Thursday through Saturday 11 a.m. to 9:30 p.m., and Sunday 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. (Sassafras Springs Vineyard).
