The carriage rides are back. 🎄🐴

Free horse-drawn carriage rides return to downtown Springdale on Friday, Dec 11 and Saturday, Dec 12, from 5 to 8 PM both nights. No tickets needed, just show up at The Star on Spring and hop on. Donations are welcome and benefit Springdale's Animal Shelter.

📍 The Star on Spring, 101 Spring St, downtown Springdale
🗓️ Fri Dec 11 and Sat Dec 12, 5 to 8 PM
🎟️ Free, no tickets needed
