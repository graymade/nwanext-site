Bentonville families, this Saturday is a big one. 🎉

[@amazeum](https://www.instagram.com/amazeum/) opens The Hangout this Saturday, a brand new 11,850-square-foot wing built for gathering, learning, and just hanging out together.

What to know:
📍 1009 Museum Way, Bentonville
🗓️ Grand opening celebration, Saturday Nov 7, 10 AM to 5 PM
☕ Headwaters Café & Lounge, open to everyone with no museum admission required, serving Onyx coffee, breakfast, and lunch
🎈 River Rooms for birthday parties, meetings, and workshops, plus The Overhang, a shaded outdoor gathering spot
🧒 An early learning center designed for ages 0 to 6, and outdoor learning areas

The grand opening brings performances from Papa Rap and the Ra-Ve Cultural Foundation, plus hands-on activities from community partners.

## More details

General museum admission is $17 for adults and children 2 and older, and free for members and children under 2 (Scott Family Amazeum).
