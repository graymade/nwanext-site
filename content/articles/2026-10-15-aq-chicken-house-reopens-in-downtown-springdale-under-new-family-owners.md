🐔 The legend is back. AQ Chicken House — an Arkansas institution since 1947 — closed its doors in 2023, and this summer it rose again in downtown Springdale. New family owners, the same world-famous chicken over-the-coals and cloverleaf rolls, and lines out the door. A generational comeback worth the drive.

📍 100 W Emma Ave, Suite 101, Springdale (downtown, next to Onyx Coffee Lab)
🕚 Sun 11 AM–3 PM · Mon–Sat 11 AM–9 PM
🐔 [@aqchickenhouse](https://www.instagram.com/aqchickenhouse/)
📸 Photo: aqchicken.com

## More details

AQ Chicken House is locally owned by Tom and Robin Lundstrum and Jacob and Gracie Lively (AQ Chicken House). It does not take reservations or to-go orders, and diners can join a virtual wait list online (AQ Chicken House).
