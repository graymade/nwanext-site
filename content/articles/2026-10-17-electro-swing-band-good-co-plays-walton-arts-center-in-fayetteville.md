🎷 The 1920s called — they want their dance floor back.

Good Co — billed by Walton Arts Center as the U.S.'s first electro-swing band — brings roaring-twenties jazz wired through modern electronic beats to Baum Walker Hall on Wednesday, Oct 28 at 7 PM. Vintage samples, live horns, and a full dance-party band from Seattle, fresh off their new album Bad Habit. Best part: it's in WAC's 10x10 Arts Series, so tickets are just $10.

📍 Walton Arts Center, 495 W Dickson St, Fayetteville
🗓 Wed Oct 28 · 7 PM · $10
🎶 [@goodcomusic](https://www.instagram.com/goodcomusic/) · [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/)

## More details

The show is billed as high-energy and improvisational, and audiences of all ages are invited to dance (Walton Arts Center). Buying tickets to three or more shows qualifies for the Create Your Own Package discount (Walton Arts Center).
