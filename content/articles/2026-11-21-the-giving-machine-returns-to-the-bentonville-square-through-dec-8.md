The Giving Machine is back on the Bentonville Square 🎄

These bright red vending machines work in reverse: instead of buying something for yourself, you buy a warm meal, a hygiene kit, school supplies, or a bike for someone else, and 100% of your donation goes to the charity you choose. They are open 24 hours a day, now through Tuesday, Dec 8.

📍 Bentonville Square, across from 21c Hotel (201 NE A St)
🕒 Open 24 hours, now through Tue Dec 8

Last year, more than 14,000 people stopped by and 8,100 items were purchased and donated.
