Bentonville, pull up a chair. The Momentary's Supper Club is back, and this month's table belongs to Arkansas native Chef Mitchell Griffith. 🍽️

It is a three-course, family-style dinner built for sharing and lingering: whipped feta with toasted sesame and chili flake, chashu pork red beans and rice, and greens, beans, and katsu half chicken.

📍 Fermentation Hall, The Momentary, 507 SE E St, Bentonville
🗓️ Wednesday, November 18
🕐 6–8 PM
🎟️ $115 ($105 for members), limited seats

Reserve through the link in [@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/)'s bio or call the box office at (479) 657-2335.
