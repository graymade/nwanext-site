Springdale, Sunday afternoon is for celebrating light. ✨

Diwali: The Festival of Lights is at the Springdale Public Library this Sunday, November 8, from 2 to 4 PM. Music, dance, refreshments, and a presentation on the traditions and meaning of Diwali, how it's celebrated in India and around the world, and the themes of light, hope, renewal, and community.

It's free, it's for all ages, and no tickets are needed. Just come by.

📍 Springdale Room, Springdale Public Library, 405 S Pleasant St, Springdale
🗓️ Sunday, November 8, 2–4 PM
🎟️ Free, no tickets needed
