Arkansas’ first Woof Gang is open in Rogers, bringing [@woofgangrogers](https://www.instagram.com/woofgangrogers/) full-service dog grooming, specialty treats, pet food, toys and spa products to 1803 S. 46th St., Suite 115.

The brand says the Rogers shop opened in June. The Rogers-Lowell Chamber has scheduled its ribbon cutting for 3 p.m. Friday, Aug. 28, giving local dog owners a new neighborhood pet boutique to explore.
