Michaels has opened its third next-generation concept store in Rogers, according to the company. The format adds dedicated areas for fabric and sewing, fine art, floral, framing and party supplies, plus a Swatch Bar, Cut Bar and upgraded event space.

Michaels says the Rogers store follows concept locations in Easton, Ohio, and Irving, Texas. Some features are planned for a wider rollout. The image is representative company press photography of the new store format, not identified as the Rogers location.
