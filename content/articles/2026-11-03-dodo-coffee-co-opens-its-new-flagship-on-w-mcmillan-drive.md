Fayetteville, your new coffee stop has a comeback story worth knowing. ☕

Dodo Coffee Co. is back in a brand new flagship home at 2568 W McMillan Dr, after its first shop closed for a road expansion project. The family-owned team spent the in-between years serving from a truck and trailer, and now they have a real home again, complete with wood-burning fireplaces and an indoor and outdoor courtyard.

What to know:
📍 2568 W McMillan Dr, Fayetteville
☕ House-made syrups, including the Maple Latte made with Vermont maple syrup
🌱 Beans roasted in Greenland, Arkansas, just south of Fayetteville

It is the kind of small, thoughtful shop that rewards a slow morning. Go say hi, grab a latte, and settle in.
