🍰 If you've ever had to pass on the birthday cake, this one's for you. Dempsey Bakery is now open in Fayetteville — a 100% dedicated gluten-free, soy-free, peanut-free, and tree nut-free bakery. Fresh breads, handcrafted sweets, café lunches, custom cakes, and take-and-bake favorites like lasagna — all made without compromise. It's the Little Rock favorite's new NWA home, in the old Arsaga's Mill District space, and regulars say you'd never know the difference.

📍 481 S School Ave, Fayetteville
