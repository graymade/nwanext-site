Lace up for a trail run with a view. 🏃‍♀️

Mount Sequoyah is hosting the Tree Topper Trail Run this Saturday, a self-timed 5K you can run or walk along the Woods Trail, out to a turnaround pavilion and back. Kids are welcome, and the finish line has snacks, coffee, hot chocolate, Bloody Marys, and mimosas waiting.

Registration is required, and proceeds support the Festival of Trees and updates to the Bailey Center.

📍 Mount Sequoyah, East Pavilion, 150 N Skyline Dr, Fayetteville
🗓️ Saturday, Nov 21, 9 to 10:30 AM
🎟️ $39.19, register on Eventbrite

[@mountsequoyah](https://www.instagram.com/mountsequoyah/)

## More details

The course is on rocky terrain, and the run starts at the East Pavilion (Mount Sequoyah).
