Rogers, get ready to eat a lot of sushi.

Little Rock favorite Mizuki A.Y.C.E. Sushi and Hibachi is opening a new Rogers location in summer 2027. It is all-you-can-eat sushi and hibachi, with rolls made fresh to order every round, plus hibachi plates like steak and shrimp.

The Little Rock flagship built a following on exactly that setup, fresh rolls and small plates, order as much as you want. Now it is Rogers turn.

No address announced yet, we will share it when it drops.
