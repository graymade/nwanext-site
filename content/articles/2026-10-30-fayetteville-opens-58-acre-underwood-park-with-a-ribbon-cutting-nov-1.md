West Fayetteville is getting a whole new place to play.

Underwood Park, the city's new 58-acre community park on the former Razorback golf course, celebrates its grand opening this Sunday, November 1, with a ribbon cutting from 2 to 4 PM. The park brings a playground, pickleball and basketball courts, walking paths, restrooms, and small pavilions to the west side of town, with connections to the Clabber Creek Trail. Come by foot or bike if you can: the entrance is on the northeast side off Deane Solomon Road, and parking is on the surrounding streets.

📍 Underwood Park, West Fayetteville (entrance off Deane Solomon Rd) · [@fayettevillear](https://www.instagram.com/fayettevillear/)
🕐 Ribbon cutting: Sun Nov 1, 2-4 PM

## More details

The northeast entrance is off 2837 N Deane Solomon Road, and the park is open from sunrise to sunset (City of Fayetteville).
