[@communitycreativecenter](https://www.instagram.com/communitycreativecenter/) is bringing a free hands-on art day to Fayetteville.

Pottery on the Patio returns Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026, from 11 AM-5 PM at Walton Arts Center’s Nadine Baum Studios, 505 W. Spring St.

Try pottery wheels, watercolor painting and kids’ clay, plus face painting and free ice cream. Free B-Unlimited shirts will feature designs printed live by local artists while supplies last.

It’s paired with the free, self-guided Fayetteville Gallery Hop.
