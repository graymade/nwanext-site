🌍 The secret's out — and it's official.

National Geographic just named the Southern Ozarks one of its Best of the World 2027 destinations. The annual list spotlights 25 places across the globe worth building a trip around — and our corner of Arkansas and Missouri made the cut.

The write-up calls out Bentonville's Crystal Bridges — fresh off its 114,000-square-foot expansion — plus the brand-new OZ Trails Bike Park (Arkansas's first chairlift-served bike park), the new 8th Street Gateway Park with its Olympic-pedigree skate park, and The Compton hotel.

📍 Bentonville, Arkansas · [@visitbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/visitbentonville/) [@crystalbridgesmuseum](https://www.instagram.com/crystalbridgesmuseum/)

## More details

The article places OZ Trails Bike Park in Bella Vista and notes that the USA Cycling National Mountain Bike Team trains in Bentonville (National Geographic). It also mentions that The Compton has a cycling concierge (National Geographic).
