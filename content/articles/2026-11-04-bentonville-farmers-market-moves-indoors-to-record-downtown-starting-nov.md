The market does not hibernate, it just moves inside. 🧺

The Bentonville Farmers Market kicks off its indoor season this Saturday at Record Downtown, from [@downtownbentonville](https://www.instagram.com/downtownbentonville/). Same producer-only promise, just warmer: fresh produce, homemade goods, sweet treats, local art, and gifts, all from the growers and makers themselves.

📍 Record Downtown, 104 SW A St, Bentonville
🗓️ Saturdays, starting November 7 through March 20, 2027
🕐 9 AM to noon
🎟️ Free and open to the public

The outdoor season wrapped October 31, so this is your Saturday morning ritual, indoors for the winter. See you there?

## More details

There is no market on Saturday, Dec. 26 (Downtown Bentonville Inc.).
