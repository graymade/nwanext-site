Your October coffee habit just got a passport. ☕

The Grounds for Good Bentonville Coffee Crawl runs all month, Oct 1–31: grab a Coffee Passport (at participating shops or via the link in [@grounds.for.good](https://www.instagram.com/grounds.for.good/)'s bio), stamp it at 10 downtown stops — Kohi, Third Space, Field Notes, Airship, the Meteor, Two Friends Books and more — and unlock $30+ in exclusive discounts and signature drinks along the way. Finish all the stamps and pick up a free tote.

Every passport purchase funds Water for Good's safe-water and sanitation work around the world. 🌍

📍 Downtown Bentonville
🗓 Oct 1–31
