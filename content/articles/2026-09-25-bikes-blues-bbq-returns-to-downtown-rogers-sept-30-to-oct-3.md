[@bikesbluesandbbq](https://www.instagram.com/bikesbluesandbbq/) returns to Downtown Rogers on Sept. 30.

Northwest Arkansas' biggest motorcycle rally brings four days of Ozark rides, live blues, and a championship BBQ cook-off to the streets of historic downtown — all raising money for NWA charities. Many attractions are free; select competitions and events may carry a fee.

📍 Downtown Rogers
🗓 Sept. 30–Oct. 3
🎟 Many attractions free, as listed by Destination Rogers.
