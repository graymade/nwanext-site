Rogers just got a fresh new lunch spot, and it is worth a trip to Pinnacle Hills.

SoFresh opened its first Arkansas location on Thursday, Oct 22, in Pinnacle Hills, next to Hotel Vin and Un Deux Trois. It is run by a local husband and wife team.

What to expect: signature and build your own bowls, salads, wraps, quesadillas and smoothies, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten free options.

📍 Pinnacle Hills, Rogers
🕐 Mon-Fri 10:30am-9pm, Sat-Sun 10:30am-8:30pm

## More details

The restaurant is at 5505 W Northgate Rd., Suite 106, in Rogers (SoFresh). Its phone number is 479-512-2285 (SoFresh).
