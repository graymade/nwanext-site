Some sad news out of Springdale.

Pleasant Counter, the family-owned comfort food spot known for its grass-fed smash burgers and beef tallow fries, is closing for good. Co-owner Ahren Boulanger says the lease is ending, and the final day of service is Wednesday, November 25.

If you have been meaning to go, this is the week. They are closed Sundays and Mondays, so every remaining service counts. Get one last double smash, a crispy chicken sandwich, and an organic sourdough fried pie while you can.

📍 907 S Pleasant St, Springdale
🗓️ Final day: Wednesday, Nov 25
🕐 Open Tuesday through Saturday, lunch and dinner

Thank you to the whole Pleasant Counter crew for feeding Springdale so well. You will be missed.
