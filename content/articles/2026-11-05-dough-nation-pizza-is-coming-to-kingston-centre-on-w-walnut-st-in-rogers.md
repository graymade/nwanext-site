Rogers, a new pizza stop is on the way, and it starts with the crust. 🍕

Dough Nation Pizza is coming soon to Kingston Centre on W Walnut St, serving crispy thin-crust tavern-style pizza, cut in squares. Their signature pie is The Original, topped with a house meat blend seasoned with seven spices, and the cheese is shredded fresh in-house off the block.

The menu also has Chicken BBQ, Baked Potato, Supreme, Taco, Chicken Bacon Ranch, and Alfredo pies, plus garlic bread, salads, and an Italian Beef sandwich. Carry-out only.

No opening day yet, they say they're close. Follow along for the date.

📍 Kingston Centre on W Walnut St, Rogers
🕐 Hours (per their site): Tue–Thu 4–10 PM · Fri–Sat 4 PM–midnight · Sun–Mon closed
