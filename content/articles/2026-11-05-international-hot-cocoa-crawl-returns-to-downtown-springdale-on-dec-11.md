Mark your calendars, Springdale. The Hot Cocoa Crawl is back. ☕

The International Hot Cocoa Crawl returns to downtown Springdale on Friday, December 11, from 5 to 8 PM. Your ticket gets you a commemorative mug and access to all the participating stops around downtown, each pouring its own international take on hot cocoa.

It's become a downtown holiday tradition, and tickets go on sale November 11 at 7 PM. Set a reminder and keep an eye on [@downtownspringdale](https://www.instagram.com/downtownspringdale/) for the ticket link and stop details as they drop.

📍 Downtown Springdale
🗓️ Friday, December 11, 5–8 PM
🎟️ Tickets on sale November 11 at 7 PM

[@downtownspringdale](https://www.instagram.com/downtownspringdale/)
