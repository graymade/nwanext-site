Burgers and Beaujolais, today in Bentonville.

Muselet is pouring a three-wine Gamay flight alongside three one-day-only smashed slider pairings, with pommes frites on the side. It is a deep dive into Beaujolais, the other beloved red grape of Burgundy, in one of the prettiest rooms in town.

📍 Muselet Champagne Bar, 406 SE 5th St Ste 4, Bentonville
🕐 Today, Sun Nov 15, 11 AM to 4 PM
🎟️ $75 per person, tickets through the link in [@muselet.nwa](https://www.instagram.com/muselet.nwa/) bio
