[@casa.alejo.nwa](https://www.instagram.com/casa.alejo.nwa/) is coming to Fayetteville.

The family-owned Tex-Mex spot — already a favorite in downtown Springdale and downtown Rogers — is building out its third location in downtown Fayetteville. They've been teasing it on Instagram with behind-the-scenes construction updates, and they started hiring the opening team back in August.
