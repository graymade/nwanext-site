Rogers, your next lake weekend has a name, and you have probably seen it on TV. ✨

The Arka Motel is a 1964 motor lodge above Beaver Lake, brought back to life in a two-part HGTV Fixer to Fabulous renovation. Twenty-four mid-century rooms, lake views, fire pits, a seasonal pool, and a private bike trail right out back. It sits about a mile from Prairie Creek Marina and five minutes from downtown Rogers, so the lake is the plan and town is right there when you want it.

📍 14733 Dutchmans Dr, Rogers
🛏️ 24 rooms, now open
🔗 Book at arkamotel.com

[@arkamotel](https://www.instagram.com/arkamotel/)

## More details

Check-in is at 3 PM and check-out is at 11 AM (Arka Motel). The motel is pet friendly and has boat parking (Arka Motel).
