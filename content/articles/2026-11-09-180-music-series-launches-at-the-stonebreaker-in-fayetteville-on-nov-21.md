Fayetteville, two of Nashville's most-played songwriters are coming to the Barn on Markham Hill. 🎶

Rebecca Lynn Howard and Rachel Thibodeau have written hits for Dolly Parton, Blake Shelton, Luke Bryan, and Tim McGraw. On Saturday, November 21, they launch the 180 Music Series at The Stonebreaker with a live-fire supper club at 6 PM, then the show in the Barn at 7:30 PM. Expect an intimate night of songs and the stories behind them.

Musicians, stick around for Sunday, November 22: the 180 Music Bridge workshop at the Fayetteville Public Library, learning the music business straight from industry insiders.

📍 The Stonebreaker, 2231 W Markham Rd, Fayetteville
🗓️ Saturday, November 21
🕐 Supper 6 PM, show 7:30 PM
