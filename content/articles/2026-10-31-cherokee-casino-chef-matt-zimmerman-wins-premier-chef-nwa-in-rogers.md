A regional chef just earned a ticket to the big leagues.

Chef Matt Zimmerman of Cherokee Casino won Premier Chef Northwest Arkansas on Saturday, October 24, at the Rogers Convention Center, taking home the title, a $1,500 travel award, and the Golden Ticket to the World Food Championships. He beat out four other local chefs, including Lasang Pinoy's Bill Martfeld, in a night that raised funds for the Rogers Public Library Foundation. Tyson Foods' Alejandra Serna-Cardona took home the Crowd Favorite award.
