Big changes are coming to the AMP in 2027.

The Walmart AMP just announced an expansion that upgrades the experience for concertgoers and artists alike. A new two-story premium hospitality complex will replace the open-air tent that has housed the Land O'Lakes Lounge since the venue opened in 2014, with climate-controlled spaces, an all-inclusive club experience, and elevated food options. Backstage, artists and crews get an expanded wing with a dedicated gym and lounge.

The work is made possible by Walmart, General Mills, Monster, Land O'Lakes, and the Walker Foundation, and construction is expected to be done in time for the 2027 Synchrony Concert Series. Same stage, same lawn, a much comfier night out.
