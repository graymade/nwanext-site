[@vinylranch](https://www.instagram.com/vinylranch/) brings its country DJ dance party to [@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/) in Bentonville on Sunday, Oct. 25.

Founder DJ [@diskocowboy](https://www.instagram.com/diskocowboy/) mixes country classics, jukebox hits and remixes on the 19th Anniversary Tour, with local opener Razorback Recking Crew.

7-10 PM
RØDE House / Arvest Bank Courtyard
Tickets: $30, or $24 for members.
