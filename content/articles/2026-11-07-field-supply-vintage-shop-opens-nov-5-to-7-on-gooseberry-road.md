Bentonville, the little vintage shop off Gooseberry Road is open again this weekend. ✨

Field Supply only opens its doors once a month, and this is that weekend. Inside the old mechanic's shop you will find racks of vintage clothing, home decor, and artwork, all hand-picked by Emily and her mom Lizzie. It is the kind of place where you go in for a look and come out with a story.

📍 Field Supply, 12770 Gooseberry Rd, Bentonville
🗓️ Thursday–Saturday, November 5–7
🕐 11 AM–5 PM
