Parade season is officially here 🎄

From the Saturday after Thanksgiving through December 12, Christmas parades are rolling through Northwest Arkansas. We pulled together the ones in our five towns so you can plan around them, and visitnwa has the full list of over a dozen across the region if you want to go further afield.

Save this one, you will want it:

Sat, Nov 28, 6 PM: Rodeo of the Ozarks Christmas Parade, E Emma Ave, Springdale
Thu, Dec 3, 6:30 PM: Lights of the Ozarks Parade, Fayetteville Square
Fri, Dec 4, 6:30 PM: Rogers Christmas Parade, downtown Rogers (Christmas Village from 5 PM at Frisco Plaza)
Sat, Dec 5: Farmington Christmas Parade, downtown Farmington
Sat, Dec 12, 6 PM: Bentonville Christmas Parade, downtown Bentonville, theme "Lights, Camera, Christmas!"

Five chances to catch Santa, marching bands, and floats lit up like the whole town plugged in. Which one are you hitting first?
