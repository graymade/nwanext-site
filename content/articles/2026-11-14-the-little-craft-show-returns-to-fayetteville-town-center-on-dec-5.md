Fayetteville, your favorite makers market is back 🎄

The Little Craft Show returns to the Fayetteville Town Center on Saturday, December 5, from 10 AM to 5 PM. It is a full day of local artists, makers, and independent shops under one roof on the downtown square, and admission is completely free.

This crew has been putting on curated maker events across Northwest Arkansas for more than a decade, and the winter show is the big one for holiday shopping: handmade gifts, original art, jewelry, prints, and plenty you will not find anywhere else. There is a free craft table for kids hosted by The Library Vintage, too.

📍 Fayetteville Town Center, 15 W Mountain St, Fayetteville
🕐 Saturday, December 5, 10 AM to 5 PM
🎟️ Free admission
