Fayetteville, your salad era has officially arrived. 🥗

[@sweetgreen](https://www.instagram.com/sweetgreen/) is now open in Fayetteville, the brand's first Arkansas location, serving the salads, warm bowls, and protein plates it built its name on. Think Harvest Bowl, Kale Caesar, Chicken Pesto Parm, and a build-your-own option, all made from scratch with whole produce delivered that morning.

📍 542 W Streamside Bend, Unit 200, Fayetteville (South Yard)
🕐 Open daily, 10:30 AM to 9 PM
📱 Order ahead for pickup or delivery, or dine in

A second Arkansas location is also open at Walmart's new Home Office campus in Bentonville, but this one is Fayetteville's own.
