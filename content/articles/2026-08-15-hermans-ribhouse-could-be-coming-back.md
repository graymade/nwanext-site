Herman’s Ribhouse could be coming back. Owner Nick Wright says he’s working to reopen the Fayetteville staple, which has been closed since spring. No reopening date has been announced.

Herman’s has operated in Fayetteville since 1964 and was inducted into the Arkansas Food Hall of Fame in 2024. Wright’s announcement drew about 1,300 reactions and 175 comments—a clear sign of how much people miss it. The ribs photo is representative, not from Herman’s.
