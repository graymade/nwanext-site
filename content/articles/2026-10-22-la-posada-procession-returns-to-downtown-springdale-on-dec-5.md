🕯️✨ Back for year two: La Posada returns to Downtown Springdale.

After an incredible first year last December, La Posada is back — a festive procession through Downtown Springdale rooted in the Mexican tradition of Las Posadas, ending at The Jones Center with live music, piñatas, crafts, food and holiday cheer. Sat Dec 5, 5:30–8 PM. Organized by The Jones Center with Downtown Springdale Alliance.

📍 Downtown Springdale → The Jones Center, Springdale · [@thejonescenter](https://www.instagram.com/thejonescenter/) [@downtownspringdale](https://www.instagram.com/downtownspringdale/)
🗓 Sat Dec 5 · 5:30–8 PM
