🎶 A free night of music at Luther George Park. Consumed By Fire — the Ward brothers — headline a free outdoor concert in downtown Springdale this Saturday, with V. Rose, Andy Chrisman of 4Him, and For The City opening. Music starts at 7 PM and runs to about 10. No tickets, no RSVP — just bring a blanket or lawn chairs; coolers and outside food are welcome. Family-friendly, all ages, presented by City Church NWA with the Downtown Springdale Alliance. Saturday evening looks clear but cool — bring a jacket. (If severe weather hits, they'll announce it on citynwa.com; light rain moves the show indoors to the church, seating limited to 250.)

📍 Luther George Park, downtown Springdale (300 Park St)
🕖 Sat Oct 17 · Music at 7 PM
