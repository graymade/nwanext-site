[@bigdiamondcomedyfestival](https://www.instagram.com/bigdiamondcomedyfestival/) is back in Bentonville Oct. 22–24.

Three days, 40 comedians, two downtown venues. Year three of Bentonville's own comedy festival takes over The Record and Bearded Goat, with headliners Jay Jurden and Chanel Ali. Founded by Sam Tallent and Raj Suresh.

📍 The Record + Bearded Goat, Bentonville
🗓 Oct. 22–24
🎟 Weekend passes and single tickets on sale now.

https://www.bigdiamondcomedyfestival.com/

## More details

A 3-day fest pass to all shows costs $199 and is limited to 20 passes (Big Diamond Comedy Festival). Loveless is also a festival venue and hosts a free local showcase on Wednesday, Oct. 21 (Big Diamond Comedy Festival).
