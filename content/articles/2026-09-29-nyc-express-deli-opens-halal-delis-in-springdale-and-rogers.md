[@nyccdel](https://www.instagram.com/nyccdel/) is bringing a taste of the Big Apple to NWA — halal style.

NYC Express Deli is now open at TWO spots: 1003 S Pleasant St in Springdale (open daily 8 AM–midnight) and 1710 W Hudson Rd in Rogers (daily 10 AM–10 PM). The menu's got the classics: the Famous Chopped chopped burger, chicken and lamb over rice, lamb gyros, falafel wraps, and all-day breakfast — plus baklava and Dubai chocolate for dessert.

📍 1003 S Pleasant St, Springdale + 1710 W Hudson Rd, Rogers
🗓 Open now at both locations
