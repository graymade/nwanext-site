Rogers, bring your best friend to paint night 🐾

Cura Osteria is hosting a Painting Your Pet paint and sip on Thursday, December 17, from 6 to 8:30 PM. Kira from [@badgirlclubart](https://www.instagram.com/badgirlclubart/) will walk you through painting your pet on an 8x10 canvas, with all supplies included. All skill levels and all ages welcome.

After you grab a ticket, email a clear photo of your pet to events.curadtr@gmail.com at least 24 hours ahead so Kira can prep your canvas. Food is available to order separately.

🎟️ $65 per person, includes 2 drinks, tickets through the link in [@curaosteria](https://www.instagram.com/curaosteria/) bio
📍 Cura Osteria, 312 S 1st St, Rogers
🗓️ Thu Dec 17, 6 to 8:30 PM

[@curaosteria](https://www.instagram.com/curaosteria/)

## More details

Cura Osteria is a family-owned Northern Italian restaurant at 312 S 1st Street in historic downtown Rogers, and its events inbox is events.curadtr@gmail.com (Cura Osteria).
