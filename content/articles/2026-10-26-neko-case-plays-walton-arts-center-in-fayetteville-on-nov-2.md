One of the great voices in American songwriting is coming to Dickson Street.

Neko Case brings her Neon Grey Midnight Green Tour to Walton Arts Center on Monday, November 2, with musical guest Destroyer. The GRAMMY-nominated singer-songwriter has spent more than 25 years building one of the most distinctive voices in indie rock and country noir, and her ninth album is her biggest-sounding and most intimate yet.

📍 Walton Arts Center, Baum Walker Hall, 495 W Dickson St, Fayetteville · [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/)
🗓 Mon Nov 2 · 7 PM
🎟 Tickets via waltonartscenter.org
