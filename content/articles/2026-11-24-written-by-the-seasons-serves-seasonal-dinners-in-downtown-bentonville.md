Bentonville has a table worth booking ahead for ✨

Written By The Seasons opened its Bentonville doors this past April, and it is the seasonally inspired spot downtown where the menu follows the market: seasonal ingredients, locally raised provisions, and dishes that change with the seasons. Dinner only, and reservations go through Resy.

It is the kind of place built for date nights and celebrations, and the Michelin Guide has listed it for 2024, 2025, and 2026. Save room for the sweet finish: their ice cream sundae and warm ginger molasses snap.

🕒 Tue to Sat, 5 to 10 PM, closed Sun and Mon
📍 215 NW A St, Suite 150, Bentonville
🎟️ Reservations through Resy, link in [@writtenbytheseasons](https://www.instagram.com/writtenbytheseasons/) bio
