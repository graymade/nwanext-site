Fayetteville, here is your Black Friday alternative. 🎨

Spare Change, the free thrift store on Township, is holding its Anti Black Friday art market this Saturday. Local artists will have original art, jewelry, woodwork, and more, and the money goes straight to the makers.

📍 Spare Change, 121 W Township St, Suite 2, Fayetteville
🗓️ Saturday, Nov 21st, noon to 6 PM
🎟️ Free to get in

Come find something handmade instead of standing in a checkout line.
