Santa is coming to brunch 🎄

Bacchus Kitchen and Bar inside Hotel Vin is hosting Santa Brunch on Sundays Dec 6, 13 and 20, with holiday favorites, photos with Santa, and a little Christmas magic. Brunch runs 10 AM to 2 PM, and Santa is part of your normal brunch reservation with no additional fee. Hotel Vin says spots are almost sold out, so reserve through OpenTable soon.

📍 Bacchus Kitchen and Bar, Hotel Vin, 5501 W Madison Ave, Rogers
🕒 Sun Dec 6, 13 and 20, 10 AM to 2 PM
🎟️ Reservations through OpenTable, link in [@bacchusnwa](https://www.instagram.com/bacchusnwa/) bio

[@bacchusnwa](https://www.instagram.com/bacchusnwa/)

## More details

Santa welcomes families in the hotel's Library, where kids can share Christmas wish lists and families can take a holiday photo (Hotel Vin Rogers). Live Christmas music plays on the Steinway grand piano during brunch, and the regular brunch menu is served (Hotel Vin Rogers).
