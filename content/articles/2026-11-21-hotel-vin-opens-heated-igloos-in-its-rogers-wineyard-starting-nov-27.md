Rogers is getting its own little winter wonderland. 🥂

Starting Friday, Nov 27, Hotel Vin is turning its WineYard into Bubbles and Bubbly, a private heated igloo experience tucked among twinkling lights and festive decor.

Each 90-minute booking is yours for up to six guests and comes with a bottle of Laurent-Perrier Champagne and a tiered charcuterie spread. There is also a seasonal menu of bites to add on, and a sparkling cider option if you would rather skip the alcohol. Kids are welcome, and it runs evenings from 5 to 9:30.

📍 Hotel Vin, 5501 W Madison Ave, Rogers
📅 Starts Fri Nov 27
💵 $200 per party, prepaid, book ahead on OpenTable
