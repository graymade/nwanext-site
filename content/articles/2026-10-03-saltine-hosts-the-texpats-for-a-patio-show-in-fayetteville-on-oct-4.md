A Sunday-night show at your favorite café. 🌙

Saltine is going After Hours this Sunday, Oct 4 at 7pm — The Texpats (Nashville-based Texas trio Grace Pettis, Rachel Laven + Natalie Price) play three-part harmony out on the patio. $25 a head, and every dollar goes to the touring musicians.

It’s BYOE — Bring Your Own Everything: chairs, blankets, drinks, snacks, friends. They bring the music and the vibe. Tickets are limited: DM [@saltinefood](https://www.instagram.com/saltinefood/) to claim yours (Venmo or cash; donations welcome).

📍 659 E Huntsville Rd, Fayetteville
🗓 Sun Oct 4 · 7pm · $25 (all to the musicians)
