Two October weekends of handmade everything in Rogers.

[@mademarketnwa](https://www.instagram.com/mademarketnwa/) is bringing its Craft Fair outdoors: Oct. 9–10 and Oct. 16–17, shop local makers right outside the market, then head inside to browse 130+ makers under one roof. Hours 9am–6pm both weekends. Halloween and holiday shopping, handled early.

📍 6361 S Oldridge Place, Rogers (Suite 108)
🗓 Oct 9–10 & Oct 16–17 · 9am–6pm
