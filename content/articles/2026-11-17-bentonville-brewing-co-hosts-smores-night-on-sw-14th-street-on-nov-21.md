Bentonville, s'mores season is officially on. 🔥

Bentonville Brewing Co. is hosting S'mores Night this Saturday, and it is built for a cozy fall evening. Grab a gourmet s'mores kit and get toasting. Bringing the little ones? Their s'mores supplies are free.

And the timing is perfect: their Roundabout Mocha Stout returns this Friday, all espresso, dark chocolate, and a touch of vanilla. Pretty much a s'more in a glass.

📍 Bentonville Brewing Co., 901 SW 14th St, Suite 100, Bentonville
🗓️ Sat Nov 21, 6 PM
🍫 Gourmet s'mores kits for purchase, free supplies for kids
