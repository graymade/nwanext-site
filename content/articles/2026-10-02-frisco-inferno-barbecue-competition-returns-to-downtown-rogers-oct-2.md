Smell that smoke? 🔥

The 11th annual Frisco Inferno Barbecue Competition takes over South First Street in downtown Rogers TODAY and tomorrow.

New this year: a Friday sauce competition (6–8pm, People's Choice Best Sauce — $5 for a plate of five sausage slices). Saturday is the main event — 12:30–3pm, $10 taster packs with five 1-oz pork + chicken samples and your vote for People's Choice. Everything sold on-site.

Hosted by the Rotary Club of Downtown Rogers.

📍 South First Street, Downtown Rogers
🗓 Fri Oct 2, 6–8pm + Sat Oct 3, 12:30–3pm · $5–$10

## More details

Saturday taster packs can be bought in advance online or at the entrance, and each includes one chicken and one pork People's Choice voting ticket (Rotary Club of Downtown Rogers).
