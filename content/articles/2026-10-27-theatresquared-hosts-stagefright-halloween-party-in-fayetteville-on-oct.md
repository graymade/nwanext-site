TheatreSquared is throwing a Halloween party, and the building itself is part of the show.

Stagefright turns T2 into a haunted theatre for one night only. There is a DJ and a dance floor, a haunted bar pouring themed cocktails and mocktails, photo ops, and costumes very much encouraged. GA tickets include food and one drink ticket. VIP is already sold out, which means the hidden Ghostlight Speakeasy, with its magician and piano bar, is spoken for. The main party is still open.

📍 TheatreSquared, 477 W Spring St, Fayetteville · [@theatresquared](https://www.instagram.com/theatresquared/)
🗓 Fri Oct 30 · 6-11 PM · 21+
🎟 GA $65 at theatre2.org · VIP $95, sold out
