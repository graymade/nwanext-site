Springdale, your favorite Latin spot just got a whole lot closer to Rogers.

Romero's Cuisine opens its second location today in Rogers, bringing the same Latin American and Colombian cooking that made the Springdale original a local favorite. If you need a first order, the Sancocho Trifasico is the one to try, a hearty three-meat soup with beef, chicken, pork, and root vegetables.
