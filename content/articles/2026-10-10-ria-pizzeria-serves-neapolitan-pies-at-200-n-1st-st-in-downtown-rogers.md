Rogers has a Neapolitan gem hiding in plain sight. 🍕

Ria Pizzeria is open in downtown Rogers — contemporary Neapolitan pies on 72-hour cold-proofed dough with Italian 00 flour and San Marzano tomatoes, plus wine, beer, and signature cocktails. The menu runs from a $22 signature pie pairing calabresa sausage with okra to a Portuguese-style pie with ham, heart of palm, and kalamata olives. No reservations — just walk in.

📍 200 N 1st St, Rogers
🕐 Sun & Mon 4–9pm · Wed–Sat 11am–10pm · Tue closed
