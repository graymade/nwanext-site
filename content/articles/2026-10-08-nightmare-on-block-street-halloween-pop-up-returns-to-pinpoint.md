🎃 Fayetteville's spookiest bar is back

Nightmare on Block Street has taken over Pinpoint — the downtown Fayetteville bar is now an immersive Halloween pop-up with themed cocktails, horror-movie booths, wall-to-wall spooky decor, and Halloween pinball machines. The crew shows up in a new group costume every weekend: Harry Potter night this Friday, Johnny Depp characters this Saturday. Dress up and join in. 🕸️

📍 Pinpoint, 23 N Block Ave, downtown Fayetteville
🗓 Through Nov 1 · 21+
🎟 Walk-in — no tickets

[@nightmareonblockstreet](https://www.instagram.com/nightmareonblockstreet/) [@pinpointfayar](https://www.instagram.com/pinpointfayar/)

## More details

The 2026 pop-up runs Sept. 25 through Nov. 1 (Nightmare on Block Street). Pinpoint opens at 4 PM, stays open until 2 AM Thursday through Saturday and is closed Mondays (Pinpoint).
