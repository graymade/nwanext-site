Soup dumplings are coming to Rogers. 🥟

San Wei is a noodle and dumpling workshop opening soon in the old Steak and Shake building in Pinnacle Hills. The name means three flavors, and the menu is built around them: steamed, pan-fried, and in a bowl. Think xiaolong bao with hot soup inside, potstickers with a crisp golden bottom, Shanghai-style pan-fried pork buns, tonkotsu ramen at five spice levels, and numbing Chongqing noodles.

No opening date yet. Their site has a signup that texts you the date when it drops, and getting on the list scores you a free bun during opening week.

📍 1715 S 46th St, Rogers (the old Steak and Shake)
🌐 sanweirogers.com
