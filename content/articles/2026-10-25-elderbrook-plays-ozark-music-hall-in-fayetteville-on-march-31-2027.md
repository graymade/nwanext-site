Spring 2027 is already looking good.

Elderbrook brings his Heart on a Wire Tour to Ozark Music Hall on Wednesday, March 31, 2027, with Arlo and Rules. The British multi-instrumentalist and producer is touring behind his new album Heart on a Wire, out January 22, and a dollar from every ticket goes to children's mental health charities through PLUS1.

📍 Ozark Music Hall, 3615 N Steele Blvd, Fayetteville · [@ozarkmusichall](https://www.instagram.com/ozarkmusichall/)
🗓 Wed Mar 31, 2027 · Doors 7 PM, show 8 PM · All ages
🎟 Tickets on sale now via Prekindle
