Fresh-roasted coffee company [@dodocoffeeco](https://www.instagram.com/dodocoffeeco/) has opened its new Dodo Cafe in Fayetteville.

The family-built company says the cafe is open Monday through Friday from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Sunday from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. A grand opening celebration is planned for Saturday, Aug. 29.

Find it at 2568 W. McMillan Drive.
