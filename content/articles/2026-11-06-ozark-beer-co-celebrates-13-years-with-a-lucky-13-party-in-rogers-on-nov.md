Rogers, your favorite brewery is turning thirteen on Friday the 13th. 🍻

Ozark Beer Co. is throwing itself a Lucky 13 birthday party, and they are leaning all the way into the superstition. Black cat adoptions, tarot readings, wood-fired pizza, and three special beer releases in the taproom, including the return of their Schwarzbier.

📍 Ozark Beer Co., 109 N Arkansas St, Rogers
🗓️ Friday, November 13
🕐 1–9 PM
🐈‍⬛ Black cat adoptions with [@nwahumanesociety](https://www.instagram.com/nwahumanesociety/)
🔮 Tarot readings with [@ethereal.sun.moon](https://www.instagram.com/ethereal.sun.moon/)
🍕 Pizza from [@wickedwoodfiredpizza](https://www.instagram.com/wickedwoodfiredpizza/)

Thirteen years of beer for the people of Arkansas. Come raise one to that.
