Last call, Fayetteville. 🍗

After nearly a decade — born as a food truck in 2017 — Dot's Hot Chicken closes its doors Saturday, Oct 24. They're down to lunch-only hours (Tue–Sat, 11am–3pm) and running through chicken fast, so go early for your final sandwich. Merch is almost gone too: $5 beer glasses, $20 hats, $9 Rippin' Reaper hot sauce.

📍 2229 E Huntsville Rd, Fayetteville
🗓 Final day Sat Oct 24 · lunch-only Tue–Sat 11am–3pm
