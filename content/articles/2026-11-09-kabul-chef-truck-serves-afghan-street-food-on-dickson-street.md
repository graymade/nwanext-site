Fayetteville, there is a little Afghan food truck on Dickson Street, and the chapli burger is the thing to order. 🍔

Aziz and his family cook Afghan street food from scratch at Kabul Chef, parked outside Puritan Coffee & Beer. The chapli burger stacks three spiced patties with Afghan sauces, and the menu runs from beef shawarma off the vertical rotisserie to mantu dumplings, bolani, and kabuli pulao. Affordable, generous, and made to order.

📍 205 W Dickson St, Fayetteville
🕐 Tue–Sun 11 AM–10 PM, Fridays 2–11 PM
