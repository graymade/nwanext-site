🥪 Fayetteville, get ready — "Farchie's" is coming.

Archie's Sandwichery — the Bentonville-born sandwich shop stacking "sammies" on locally baked Italian loaves — just announced a second location on South School Avenue in Fayetteville. The lease is signed (per the shop's own Oct 20 announcement), and the new shop is coming soon. No opening date yet — and the original Bentonville shop keeps slinging.
