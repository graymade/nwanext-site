Plan change for Sunday, Springdale. Please read before you head out. 🇺🇸

The Springdale Veterans Celebration is moving indoors. Our earlier post pointed you to Luther George Park, so do not go there. The celebration now takes place inside Pat Walker Theater at Springdale High School.

Same day, same time, same free afternoon honoring our local veterans, just under a roof. Live music from The Atlantics and Dead Horse Mountain Band, a headlining set from Gavin Sumrall and the Band, food vendors, and a closing tribute to those who have served.

📍 Pat Walker Theater, Springdale High School, 1103 W Emma Ave, Springdale
🗓️ Sun Nov 15, 2 to 7 PM
🎟️ Free admission, rain or shine
