Frost Fest tickets are live, Fayetteville 🍻

The Yeti is coming back. Frost Fest returns Saturday, February 6, 2027, and this one is a milestone: the 10th festival Fossil Cove Brewing Co. has thrown since 2016, and it is the biggest day in Arkansas craft beer. The Washington County Fairgrounds turns into a one-afternoon sampling party with 50+ breweries from across the region pouring 150+ beers, including plenty you cannot normally find in Arkansas, plus live music, local vendors, artists and food trucks.

Two main tiers, and both are expected to sell out:
❄️ Early Entry, $100: in at 1 PM with a slightly larger sampling glass, shorter lines and first access to the limited releases
❄️ General Admission, $70: in at 2 PM with a commemorative sampling glass. Heads up, GA goes up to $80 on New Year's Day
❄️ Designated Driver, $55: the full festival, just no sampling

It is outdoors and goes rain, snow or shine, so dress like February in the Ozarks. 21 and up only.

📍 Washington County Fairgrounds, 2537 N McConnell Ave, Fayetteville
🗓️ Sat Feb 6, 2027, 1 to 6 PM
