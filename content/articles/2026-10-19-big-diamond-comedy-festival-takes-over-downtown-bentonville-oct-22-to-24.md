🎤😂 Bentonville's comedy festival is back — and it starts THIS Thursday.

The Big Diamond Comedy Festival takes over downtown Bentonville Oct 22–24, with shows at Record Downtown (104 SW A St) and Bearded Goat. On the bill: Rory Scovel headlining Friday night at the Record (10 PM), plus Chanel Ali, Learnmore Jonasi, Cam Bertrand, Jay Jurden, Moms Unhinged, a live D&D comedy show, and co-founder Sam Tallent's own Comedy-Rasslin' Circus (Sat 1 PM, $15) and Saturday late show. Also officially on the fest bill: Shane Mauss Presents: Trip Tales! — psychedelic storytelling, Fri Oct 23 at 8 PM (18+; $20 advance, $40 at the door — buy early). Tickets on Eventbrite, including a 3-day VIP pass for $199.

📍 Record Downtown + Bearded Goat, Bentonville
🗓 Thu Oct 22 – Sat Oct 24
🎟 Tickets via Eventbrite · [@bigdiamondcomedyfestival](https://www.instagram.com/bigdiamondcomedyfestival/)

## More details

A free local showcase at Loveless opens the festival on Wed., Oct. 21 at 8 PM (Big Diamond Comedy Festival). The 3-day pass to all shows is limited to 20 (Big Diamond Comedy Festival).
