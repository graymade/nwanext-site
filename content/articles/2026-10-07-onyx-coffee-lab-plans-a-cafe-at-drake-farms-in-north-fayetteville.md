Big coffee news, Fayetteville. ☕

Onyx Coffee Lab — the Rogers roaster just crowned the No. 1 coffee shop in the WORLD — is the first commercial tenant announced for Drake Farms, the $1B mixed-use development rising in north Fayetteville across from Washington Regional. The new café will be a nearly 4,800-square-foot space with outdoor seating, per the announcement. No opening date yet — it's still going up — but when the world's best coffee shop picks your development for its next café, that's a statement.

🗓 Opening date TBA — under construction
