Bentonville, your weekend holiday shopping just got a lot more fun 🎄

Smells Like Snow Winter Market is taking over Turning Pointe Dance Center on Saturday, December 5, from 9 AM to 5 PM and Sunday, December 6, from 9 AM to 3 PM. Shop NWA makers, artists, crafters, local boutiques, and small businesses, plus a themed baskets fundraiser, photo spots, and free children's activities.

🎄 $5 admission, 12 and under free
📍 900 SE Village Loop, Bentonville
🗓️ Sat Dec 5, 9 AM to 5 PM and Sun Dec 6, 9 AM to 3 PM
