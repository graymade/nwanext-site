Halloween at a museum just works.

Crystal Bridges is bringing back Trick Art Treat on Saturday, October 31. Families can trick-or-treat along the North Forest Trail, meet costumed characters, catch some live music, and make a little art along the way. Costumes are welcome but not required, and the whole thing is free, no tickets needed.

📍 Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art, 600 Museum Way, Bentonville · [@crystalbridgesmuseum](https://www.instagram.com/crystalbridgesmuseum/)
🗓 Sat Oct 31 · 11 AM-3 PM · Free, no tickets required
🚗 Parking tip from the museum: use Campus Parking, near the North Forest entrance
