Fayetteville, your unused outdoor gear is about to get a second life. 🎒

Next Gear Outdoors is taking shape on South School Ave, right next to Fossil Cove Brewing. It is a consignment shop and community hub built on a simple idea: buy, sell, and get outside. Quality used bikes, camping and backpacking gear, jackets, boots, and more will fill the shelves, all of it passed along by people right here in Northwest Arkansas.

The shop is not open for retail just yet, but intake is wide open. They are stocking up on fall and winter gear right now, with cycling gear, jackets and layers, and hiking footwear high on the list. Drop yours off on a weekday or email info@nextgearoutdoors.com to set up a time, and you earn when your gear sells.

📍 1221 S School Ave, Fayetteville
🕐 Gear intake Mon to Fri, 10 AM to 4 PM
🔜 Retail opening soon, date to come
