Your holiday shopping can start small and local this Saturday 🎁

The Momentary is hosting its Winter Holiday Art Market on Saturday, November 28, from 10 AM to 4 PM in RØDE House. More than 20 local and regional merchants will be there with a wide range of unique, giftable goods. Free to walk in, no tickets needed.

🎁 Free, no tickets required
📍 RØDE House, The Momentary, 507 SE E St, Bentonville
🗓️ Sat Nov 28, 10 AM to 4 PM
