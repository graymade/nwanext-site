Free plans for next Thursday: [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/) is opening its doors for a 35th-anniversary season preview in Fayetteville on Aug. 27 from 4:30 to 6:45 p.m.

Programming staff will give short season overviews at 5 and 6 p.m. Guests can also get help with digital tickets, learn about volunteer and local crew opportunities, browse upcoming cocktail classes, and visit the Levon Helm exhibition before it closes Sept. 6.

Admission is free, but a reservation is required through Walton Arts Center.
