Mediterranean restaurant [@tazikis](https://www.instagram.com/tazikis/) has opened its new Rogers location at 1771 W. Pleasant Grove Road, Suite 104.

The approximately 2,900-square-foot restaurant seats 85 guests and represents a reported $950,000 investment. It will employ about 22 full- and part-time staff. The restaurant is operated by Northwest Arkansas resident Brad Compton and is the brand’s third location in the region, joining Fayetteville and Bentonville.
