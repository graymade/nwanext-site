☕ It's crawling time, Fayetteville! 🍂

The Fayetteville Fall Coffee Crawl starts TODAY and runs through Monday, Oct 12. Grab a free punch card at any of the 8 participating shops, then sip and stamp your way through Basecamp, Confident, Dodo, Old Pine, Puritan, Saltine, Sicarú, and Word.

A different crawl from the Bentonville one — this one's all Fayetteville, all weekend, and free to play.

📍 Fayetteville, AR
🗓 Oct 8 – 12
🎟 Free to participate
