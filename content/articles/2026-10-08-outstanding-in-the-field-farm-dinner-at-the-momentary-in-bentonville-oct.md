Dinner in a field, white tablecloths, one very long table. 🌾

Outstanding in the Field — the traveling farm-dinner crew that's been setting that famous long table since 1999 — brings it to the Momentary this Saturday, Oct 10. Guest chef Micah Klasky of The Hive at 21c plates a multi-course, family-style meal with beverage pairings (Mountain Valley Spring Water pouring), starting with a welcome reception at 3 PM. Tickets are $390 — the splurge dinner of the season — and seats are still available.

📍 The Momentary, Bentonville
🗓 Sat Oct 10 · 3–7:30 PM
🎟 $390 via the Momentary
