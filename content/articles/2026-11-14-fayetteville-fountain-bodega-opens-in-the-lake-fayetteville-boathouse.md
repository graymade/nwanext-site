There is a new reason to stop at Lake Fayetteville.

Fayetteville Fountain is a new bodega in the reopened boathouse at the marina, pouring coffee and serving a rotating menu of grab-and-go sandwiches, charcuterie, and pastries, all made in-house by a local pastry chef trained in Switzerland. The orange danish is the one to try first.

📍 Lake Fayetteville Marina, 1330 E Lake Fayetteville Rd, Fayetteville
🕐 Tue to Thu 12 to 5 PM, Fri and Sat 10 AM to 5 PM, closed Sun and Mon
Open today until 5 PM.
