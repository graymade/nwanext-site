A 20-year-old turned a former car wash into Bentonville's newest drive-thru coffee stop.

[@tristar.3](https://www.instagram.com/tristar.3/) is open at 3510 SE L St in Bentonville — a drive-thru-only coffee and crepe shop in the old car wash building behind Murphy USA, in front of Dairy Queen. Owner Sheccid Toledo, 20, originally dreamed of a coffee truck before finding the car wash and changing course. The menu runs 7 specialty lattes, 6 matchas, 4 smoothies, energy drinks and sparklers, plus sweet and savory crepes — with star-themed names like the First Star Latte and 3 Estrellas Spanish Latte. Open 5am–7pm daily.

📍 3510 SE L St, Bentonville (drive-thru only)
