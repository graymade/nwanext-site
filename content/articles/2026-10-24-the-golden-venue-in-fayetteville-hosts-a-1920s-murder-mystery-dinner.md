Tomorrow night, dinner comes with a mystery.

The Golden Venue in Fayetteville is hosting "The Double-Cross" on Sunday, Oct 25, from 5:30 to 7:30pm. Cocktail hour starts at 5:30, dinner follows at 6:30.

The setup: Prohibition is the law, the real party is always behind a locked door, and a toast to a hometown Hollywood star turns into the scene of the crime. Every guest gets a character role, and the night is led with Lost Valley Mysteries.

🎟 $125, which covers your ticket, food, drink, gratuity and tax (plus Eventbrite fees). Premium character roles are an extra $20, limited to 5.
📍 The Golden Venue, 2630 E Citizens Dr Ste 20, Fayetteville
🗓 Sun Oct 25, 5:30-7:30pm · 21+ · 1920s attire recommended · free parking
