Fayetteville dog owners, this one is for you and your pup.

Wagbar is bringing its off-leash dog park and neighborhood bar to Fayetteville next summer. It will be the concept's 15th location around the country.

Here is the idea: your dog gets a secure off-leash park to run and play, and you get a bar serving food and drinks right there with them. No dog? No problem, you are welcome anyway.

It will sit just off N College Ave near the NWA Mall, between the Razorback Greenway and Clear Creek.

📍 Near N College Ave, Fayetteville, coming summer 2027
📲 [@wagbar.dogpark](https://www.instagram.com/wagbar.dogpark/)

## More details

Wagbar Fayetteville is led by Arkansas natives Adam and Sara (Wagbar). It plans to offer memberships and day passes for dogs, and people get in free with or without a dog (Wagbar).
