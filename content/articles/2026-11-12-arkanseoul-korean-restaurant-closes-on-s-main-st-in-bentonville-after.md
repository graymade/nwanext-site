A Bentonville favorite has closed its doors.

ArkanSeoul, the family-owned Korean spot on S Main St, has closed permanently after six years. Since October 2020 it has been the place for crispy Korean fried chicken, galbi bowls, kimbap, and Korean corn dogs, and regulars swear by that fried chicken.
