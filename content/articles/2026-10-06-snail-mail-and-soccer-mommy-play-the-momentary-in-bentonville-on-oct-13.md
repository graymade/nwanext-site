Two of indie's biggest names, one intimate room. 🎸

Snail Mail and Soccer Mommy are bringing their co-headline S&M Tour to RØDE House at the Momentary on Tuesday, Oct 13 — the Bentonville stop on a 19-date fall run. Lindsey Jordan's touring behind her new album Ricochet; Sophie Allison behind Evergreen. Doors 6:30 PM, show 7 PM — and the Momentary says tickets are limited, so don't sleep on this one.

📍 RØDE House at the Momentary, Bentonville
🗓 Tue Oct 13 · 7 PM (doors 6:30)
🎟 $49 GA / $30 student via the Momentary box office

[@themomentary](https://www.instagram.com/themomentary/)

## More details

The Momentary is at 507 SE E St in Bentonville (The Momentary). Members paid $39 for general admission, and Premium Group Seating for four with a reserved table and table service was $490 (The Momentary).
