Rogers, your weekend plans just got a whole lot sweeter.

Playa Bowls opens its first Arkansas location this Saturday, November 14, at 11 AM in the Whole Foods shopping center at Summit at Pinnacle Springs. Acai bowls, pitaya bowls, smoothies, cold brew, and oatmeal, all from the surf-shop-inspired chain with around 400 locations nationwide.

Get there early: the first 50 guests get a free acai bowl and a custom Playa t-shirt.

📍 1803 S 46th St, Suite 134, Rogers
🕐 Grand opening Sat Nov 14, 11 AM, then daily 8 AM to 8 PM
