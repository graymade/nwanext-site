Halloween week is here, and the forecast has a plot twist.

The week itself is beautiful, sunny and in the 70s. But rain rolls in Friday night, and Halloween day is looking wet. Here is the weekend, day by day, as of Tuesday:

🎃 Fri Oct 30: High 76°, mostly cloudy and dry for Friday plans. Rain chances build overnight, up to about 40%.
🎃 Sat Oct 31: High 71°, with showers and thunderstorms likely. Trick-or-treat hours look mild, around 70° at 5 PM sliding into the mid 60s by 8, and the rain chance starts near 50% early evening before easing to about 25% by 7 PM. No heavy coats needed, but a costume that works with an umbrella or poncho is the smart call.
🎃 Sun Nov 1: High 71° and partly sunny, the dry day of the weekend if your plans can slide.

Saturday night bottoms out around 55°, and a weekend forecast can still shift between now and then, so give it one more look before you head out.
