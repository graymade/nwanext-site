Downtown Rogers has a proper Irish pub, and it's hiding in plain sight on 2nd Street. ☘️

Kilroy Public House took over the old Levi's Gastrolounge space and leans all the way into the pub classics: the Duchess' Shepherd's Pie, bangers and mash, and stout beer cheese with house soda bread for dipping.

It's the kind of spot that works for a pint after work, a long dinner, or weekend brunch, which they pour and plate on Saturday and Sunday mornings.

📍 224 S 2nd St, Rogers
🍽️ Shepherd's Pie $14 · Bangers & Mash $15 · Stout Beer Cheese $9
🥂 "A toast to kindred spirits," as they put it
