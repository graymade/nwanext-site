The Benton County Fair is back in Bentonville.

The 122nd annual fair runs Sept. 25 through Oct. 3 at the fairgrounds — gate admission ($10–$15) includes unlimited carnival rides, and parking is free. This year's highlights: a Guinness World Record hot-pepper attempt (25 Carolina Reapers) on opening night, the Jr. Livestock Auction on Oct. 1, the youth talent contest, a petting zoo, and the closing-night Two-Stepping dance on Oct. 3.

📍 7640 SW Regional Airport Blvd, Bentonville
🗓 Sept. 25 – Oct. 3
