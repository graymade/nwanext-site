Springdale, there is a new pizza truck in town, and it is worth the trip.

Zio Spency's Pizza is parked outside Olde Tyme Donuts on S Thompson, serving classic Neapolitan-style pies from a little yellow truck. The Spicy Zio brings pepperoni, fresh jalapeño, and hot honey, and La Dea (the goddess) is an olive oil base with zucchini, yellow squash, cherry tomato, and red onion. Pies run $10 to $16.

Grab a slice, then step inside the taproom for a beer while you wait.

📍 2403 S Thompson St, Springdale
🕐 Tue to Sat, 11 to 2 and 5 to 9
