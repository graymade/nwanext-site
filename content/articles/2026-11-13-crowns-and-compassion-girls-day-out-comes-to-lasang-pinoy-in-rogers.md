Rogers, grab your girls and your tiaras. 👑

Lasang Pinoy and Olivia Frank and Co. are teaming up for Crowns and Compassion, a girls day out with fashion, food, and giving back. Your ticket includes a styled Filipino grazing table, a twilly scarf to take home plus a group styling session, three rounds of bingo with a blackout finale, and time to shop Olivia's Vault. You also get a $10 Lasang Pinoy certificate for a future meal, and there is a secret mission if you are feeling brave.

📍 Lasang Pinoy, 719 W Walnut St, Suite 104, Rogers
🗓️ Sun Dec 6, 3 to 6 PM
🎟️ $45, tickets through the link in [@lasangpinoynwa](https://www.instagram.com/lasangpinoynwa/)'s bio
📲 [@lasangpinoynwa](https://www.instagram.com/lasangpinoynwa/) [@oliviafrankandco](https://www.instagram.com/oliviafrankandco/)
