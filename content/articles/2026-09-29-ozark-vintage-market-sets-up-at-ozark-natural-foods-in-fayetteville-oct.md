[@ozarkvintagemarket](https://www.instagram.com/ozarkvintagemarket/) is taking over the co-op patio this weekend.

The monthly vintage market pops up at [@ozarknaturalfoods](https://www.instagram.com/ozarknaturalfoods/) Saturday, Oct. 3 and Sunday, Oct. 4 in Fayetteville — racks and racks of vintage clothing, accessories, and more from some of the best vintage dealers in the state, with a heavy 90s–Y2K focus. Standard market hours run 11–5 both days; check their page for any game-day changes.

📍 1554 N College Ave, Fayetteville (Ozark Natural Foods)
🗓 Sat Oct 3 – Sun Oct 4
