Bentonville, the museum is turning fifteen and the party is free. 🎂

Crystal Bridges is celebrating 15 years with a birthday p(ART)y, and everyone is invited. Make a birthday tote, join the scavenger hunt, watch a mini pony get drawn live, catch DJ Girlfriend in the Main Lobby, and plenty more across the museum all afternoon. No tickets, just show up.

📍 Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art, 600 Museum Way, Bentonville
🗓️ Sunday, November 15
🕐 11 AM–4 PM
🎟️ Free, no tickets required
