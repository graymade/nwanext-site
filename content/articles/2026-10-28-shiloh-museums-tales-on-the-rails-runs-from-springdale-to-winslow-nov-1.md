Peak fall color looks even better from a train window.

The Shiloh Museum of Ozark History is running Tales on the Rails this Sunday, November 1: a ride on the Arkansas and Missouri Railroad from the Springdale Depot to Winslow and back, right through the Boston Mountains at peak foliage. Museum historian Rachel Whitaker will be aboard talking historic landmarks and railroad history, plus a program about Winslow's "petticoat government," and there will be prizes along the way. Snacks and drinks are included.

📍 Departs Springdale Depot, Springdale
🗓 Sun Nov 1 · Departs 2 PM, returns around 5:30 PM
🎟 $65 for museum members · $90 for non-members · Register at shilohmuseum.org
