Farmington has a new stop for popcorn and soda 🍿

Mountainside Artisan Popcorn and Soda Shoppe is holding its grand opening on Saturday, November 28, from 10 AM to 4 PM at 56 Yukon Way. The shop soft-opened earlier this month, and Saturday is the official celebration. Expect small-batch gourmet popcorn in more than 30 flavors, plus lemonades, dirty sodas, and decadent cold brews.

🍿 Grand opening celebration
📍 56 Yukon Way, Farmington
🗓️ Sat Nov 28, 10 AM to 4 PM
