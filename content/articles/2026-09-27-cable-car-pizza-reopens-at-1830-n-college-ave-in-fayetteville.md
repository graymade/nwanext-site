Cable Car Pizza is back in Fayetteville.

Eleven years after it closed, the longtime favorite is back at 1830 N College Ave — the same space where it first opened in 1988. Former employee TJ Claggett is reviving the shop with its original-style crust, familiar recipes, and new options for diners with dietary restrictions.

📍 1830 N College Ave, Fayetteville
🗓 Soft opened Sept. 2
