Springdale has a real ramen shop, and if you haven't been yet, this is your sign. 🍜

Menya Kitakara brings Hokkaido-style ramen to downtown Springdale, inspired by the ramen shops of Sapporo. The Sapporo miso is the one to start with, but the spicy butter miso, Hakata tonkotsu, tantanmen, and tsukemen are all worth working through. Most bowls are $15, and the gyoza and karaage are worth adding to the table.

It's run by chef and owner Inggit Rianto, who previously opened Ramen Nara in Rogers.

📍 202 E Emma Ave, Suite 102, Springdale
🕐 Lunch 11 AM–2 PM, dinner from 5 PM, Monday–Saturday. Closed Sundays.
