Come dig. 🎶

Record Pharmacy is popping up on the patio at Ozark Natural Foods on Sunday, Oct 25, 12–5 PM — crates of quality used LPs spanning soul, jazz, folk, country, blues, rock, disco and plenty more. [@ozarkmusichall](https://www.instagram.com/ozarkmusichall/), Swink Booking, HopOut DIY and [@georgesmajesticlounge](https://www.instagram.com/georgesmajesticlounge/) will have tables with show posters and upcoming-show info, and drinks, snacks and lunch are inside the co-op. Free to browse.

📍 Ozark Natural Foods patio, Fayetteville
🕐 Sunday, Oct 25 · 12–5 PM
