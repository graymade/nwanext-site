Saturday mornings are about to get a lot cozier. 🥣

The Momentary is bringing back Saturday Morning Cartoons, starting Saturday, Nov 28 in Fermentation Hall. Classic cartoons on the big screen, cozy lounges to sink into, and a cereal bar with drinks for all ages. Pajamas encouraged, not required.

Free, no tickets needed, just show up. The series runs select Saturdays through January, moving to RODE House after the first date.

📍 The Momentary, 507 SE E St, Bentonville
🗓️ Saturdays, 8:45 AM to 12 PM
🎟️ Free, no tickets required

Nov 28 (Fermentation Hall), Dec 19, Jan 9, Jan 16, Jan 23, Jan 30 (RODE House)
