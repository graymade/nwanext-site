Bentonville, martini hour is about to get an upgrade. 🍸

Vesper is coming soon to downtown, an intimate cocktail bar built around martini culture and golden-era opulence. Expect imaginative originals, timeless classics done right, and luxurious shareables made for the table. It is the sister concept to Callisto, the speakeasy right next door.

No opening date has been announced yet. Join the opening guest list at vesper.bar for first access to opening events.
