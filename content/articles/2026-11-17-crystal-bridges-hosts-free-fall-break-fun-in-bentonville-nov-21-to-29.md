School is out and Crystal Bridges is the move 🍂

Fall Break Fun runs Saturday, Nov 21 through Sunday, Nov 29, with free drop-in activities every day from 11 AM to 4 PM. Think artmaking in the studios, a scavenger hunt through the galleries, a sensory room, and a family movie marathon, plus something different each day like caricatures, tote printing, a bug roadshow, and princess visits.

📍 Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art, 600 Museum Way, Bentonville
🎟️ Free, no tickets needed
🗓️ Closed Tuesday, Nov 24 and Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26

Round up the crew and make a day of it. The trails are pretty this time of year too.

## More details

Pictures with Santa run from noon to 3 PM on Saturday, Nov. 21, in The Commons (Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art). The UARK Ento-Roadshow and a yoga obstacle course run from 1 to 4 PM on Sunday, Nov. 22, at North Landing (Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art).
