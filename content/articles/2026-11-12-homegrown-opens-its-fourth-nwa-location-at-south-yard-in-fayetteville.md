Fayetteville, your mornings are about to get a whole lot better.

HomeGrown opens its fourth Northwest Arkansas spot at South Yard on Wednesday, November 18, with doors opening at 6:30 AM. Expect the breakfast, brunch, and lunch you already know from their Springdale, Rogers, and Bentonville spots, all locally sourced and seasonal, plus homemade pastries, fresh-squeezed juices, mimosas, and Bloody Marys.

📍 South Yard, 417 W MLK Jr Blvd, Fayetteville
🕐 Opens Wed Nov 18, 6:30 AM, then daily 6:30 AM to 2:30 PM
