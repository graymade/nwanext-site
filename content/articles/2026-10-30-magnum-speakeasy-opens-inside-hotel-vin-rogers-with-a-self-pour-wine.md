Rogers has a new speakeasy worth dressing up for.

Magnum Speakeasy is now pouring inside Hotel Vin Rogers, and the draw is the wine wall: 16 wines on a self-pour system, including rare finds and limited-production bottles, in 1, 3, or 5 ounce pours so you can taste around before committing to a glass. Add in signature cocktails, premium spirits, low lighting, and plush seating, and it is an easy pick for a date night or a nightcap.
