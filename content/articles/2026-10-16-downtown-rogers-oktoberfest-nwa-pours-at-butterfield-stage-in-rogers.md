🍺 Prost, Rogers. The 14th annual Downtown Rogers Oktoberfest NWA takes over Butterfield Stage this Saturday — around 25 regional breweries pouring, plus local home brewers, wine options, German food for purchase, and live music from Fuzzy Logik and Polka, Polka! There's a ceremonial keg tapping of an event-exclusive brew, a costume contest, stein hoist, Big Wheel competition, and games. VIP gets in at 1 PM ($80, first crack at small-batch pours plus a VIP-only mixed-drink bar, meals, desserts, and shaded seating); GA is 2–6 PM ($70, sample cup + unlimited tastings). Every stein helps fund downtown Rogers impact — hosted by the Rotary Club of Downtown Rogers, the Optimist Club of Rogers, and Rotary Bentonville Connect.

📍 Butterfield Stage, Railyard Park — 101 E Cherry St, Rogers
🕐 Sat Oct 17 · VIP 1 PM / GA 2–6 PM
