[@fayettevillefilmfest](https://www.instagram.com/fayettevillefilmfest/) returns September 16-19 for four days of independent films, filmmaker conversations, panels and workshops.

The 17th annual festival brings filmmakers, industry guests and movie fans together in Fayetteville. Tickets, passes and the current schedule are available through [@fayettevillefilmfest](https://www.instagram.com/fayettevillefilmfest/)'s official site.

Details: fayettevillefilmfest.org
