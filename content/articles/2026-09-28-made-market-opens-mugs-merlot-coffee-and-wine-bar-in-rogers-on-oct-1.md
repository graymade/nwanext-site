[@mademarketnwa](https://www.instagram.com/mademarketnwa/) is pouring something new in Rogers.

Mugs & Merlot Coffee + Wine Bar — Made Market's own in-house concept — opens Thursday, Oct. 1 inside the market at 6361 S Oldridge Place. It's a sip-while-you-shop bar: hot coffees, cold drinks, dirty sodas, teas, and wine to browse the market with.

📍 6361 S Oldridge Pl, Rogers (inside Made Market)
🗓 Opens Oct. 1

## More details

Made Market is open Tuesday through Sunday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. (Made Market).
