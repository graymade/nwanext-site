Rogers has a quiet little hideaway worth the drive. ☕

Reverie Coffee and Teahouse sits out on Highway 12, and it feels like a deep breath the second you walk in. Coffee, tea, pastries, and light bites, with a cozy counter and shelves full of local goods. Owner Gina and the team keep it small and personal.

Try the vanilla bean iced latte topped with Kyoto matcha, or the blackberry smash if tea is more your speed.

📍 Reverie Coffee and Teahouse, 14438 E Hwy 12, Suite B, Rogers
🗓️ Tue-Sat, 6:30 AM to 1 PM
