Need a laugh next Friday? Springdale has you covered.

Ashley Austin Morris returns to the Jones Center's Center Stage Comedy series on Friday, November 20, at 7 PM. You have seen her on Elsbeth, Inventing Anna, and The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, and her album Slightly Off debuted at No. 1 on iTunes. It is clean comedy, built for date night or a night out with friends.

Parents' Night Out childcare is available onsite, so you can actually enjoy the show.

📍 The Jones Center, 922 E Emma Ave, Springdale
🕐 Fri Nov 20, 7 PM
🎟️ $28, $23 for members, taxes and fees included
