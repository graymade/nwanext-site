If downtown Rogers has not given you a date-night idea lately, here is one. 🍝

Cura Osteria is a family-owned neighborhood restaurant in historic downtown Rogers, rooted in the cooking traditions of Northern Italy. Think handmade pasta, seasonal dishes, Italian wines, cocktails, and spritzes, served in a relaxed room where there is no required number of courses and no dress code.

What to know:
📍 312 S 1st St, Rogers
🍽️ Dinner Wednesday through Saturday, 5 to 9 PM
🥗 Saturday lunch, 11 AM to 2 PM
🍷 Walk-ins welcome as space allows, reservations encouraged on Friday and Saturday evenings

Come for one bowl of pasta, or stay for the whole progression, antipasti to dolci. Either way works here.

## More details

Cura Osteria takes calls at (479) 340-0981, and reservations can be made through its website (Cura Osteria).
