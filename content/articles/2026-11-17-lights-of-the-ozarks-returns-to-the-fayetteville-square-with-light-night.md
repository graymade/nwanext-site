The Square is about to glow again ✨

Lights of the Ozarks returns to the Historic Downtown Square in Fayetteville, with more than 500,000 lights turning downtown into a winter wonderland for the season.

It all starts with Light Night on Friday, Nov 20 at 6 PM, when the switch flips and the Square lights up for the first time this year.

After opening night, the lights shine nightly from 5 PM to 1 AM through Jan 1. Bundle up, grab a hot cocoa and take a slow stroll around the Square. Admission is free.

Save this date too:
🎄 Lights of the Ozarks Parade: Thursday, Dec 3 at 6:30 PM around the Square

And here is a fun one: the display is nominated for USA Today 10Best Best Public Holiday Lights Display. You can vote once a day through Monday, Nov 30 at 11 AM to help Fayetteville land in the top 10.

Hosted by [@fayettevillear](https://www.instagram.com/fayettevillear/)

## More details

Visits with Santa run from 6 to 8 PM on the Square on Fridays, Dec. 4, 11 and 18 (Experience Fayetteville). Holiday movie nights start at 6 PM on Saturdays, Dec. 5, 12 and 19 (Experience Fayetteville).
