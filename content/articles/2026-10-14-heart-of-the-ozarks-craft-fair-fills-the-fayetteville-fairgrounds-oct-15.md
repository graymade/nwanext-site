🍂 Craft-fair weekend starts TOMORROW. The Heart of the Ozarks Craft Fair & Fall Festival takes over the Washington County Fairgrounds Thu–Sat, Oct 15–17, 9–6 daily: 250+ indoor & outdoor booths, food trucks, live bluegrass, a petting zoo with pony & camel rides, face painting, inflatables, and craft demos. Admission is FREE — $5 parking.

📍 Washington County Fairgrounds, 2537 N McConnell Ave, Fayetteville
