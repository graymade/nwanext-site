The holidays are coming to Baum Walker Hall, and they are bringing aerialists with them. ✨

Cirque Dreams Holidaze returns to [@waltonartscenter](https://www.instagram.com/waltonartscenter/) December 4 through 6, reimagined for 2026 with original music, beloved holiday favorites sung live, new sets, and dazzling costumes. Expect a Broadway-style production wrapped around contemporary circus arts, with holiday characters coming to life on stage.

What to know:
📍 Baum Walker Hall, 495 W Dickson St, Fayetteville
🗓️ Fri Dec 4, 7 PM
🗓️ Sat Dec 5, 11 AM and 3 PM
🗓️ Sun Dec 6, 2 PM
🎟️ Tickets from $46 via Walton Arts Center

Four shows, one very festive weekend. A good one to put on the calendar early.
