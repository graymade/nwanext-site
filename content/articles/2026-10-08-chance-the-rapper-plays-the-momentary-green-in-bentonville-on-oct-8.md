🎤 Chance the Rapper hits the Green TONIGHT 🎶

Chance brings his Coloring Book 10 Year Anniversary Tour to the Momentary Green tonight — gates open at 6 PM, show at 7:30 PM, with opener La Reezy. Standing room only, cashless, clear-bag only. The Momentary campus closes at 2 PM today (the Green at 11 AM) to get stage-ready.

Tickets are still available in limited quantities, and students get in with a valid ID. Reserve online or call the Box Office at (479) 657-2335.

📍 Momentary Green — 507 SE E St, Bentonville
🗓 Tonight, Thu Oct 8 — gates 6 PM, show 7:30 PM
🎟 Reserve online or (479) 657-2335

## More details

General admission was $85, $68 for members and $47 for students with a valid ID (The Momentary). Premium tickets added an outdoor tented lounge, a premium bar, complimentary food and a reserved front-row standing zone (The Momentary).
