Welcome. Northwest Arkansas is five cities that run together along I-49, so most people end up living in one, working in another and eating in all of them. Here's where to start.

## Get a library card

Every city library on our list offers free cards to residents, plus kids' programs, meeting rooms and events. It's one of the easiest ways to meet people. See [Libraries](/guides/#libraries).

## Find your city hall

Trash and recycling days, parks, permits and council meetings all live on your city's site. See [City halls](/guides/#city-halls).

## Learn the Greenway

The Razorback Greenway connects the region by trail. Plenty of locals bike or walk it to coffee, work and the farmers market. It's the best way to see how the cities connect.

## Know what's opening

New restaurants and shops open every month. Follow [@nwa_next](https://www.instagram.com/nwa_next/) or get [the weekly email](/newsletter/) and you'll hear about them before your coworkers do.

## Plan a weekend

Start with the visitor guides under [Things to do](/guides/#things-to-do), then check our latest [events](/news/?category=Events).
