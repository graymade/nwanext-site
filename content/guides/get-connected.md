The fastest way to feel at home in NWA is to show up to things. A few good places to start:

## Show up to free events

A lot of what we cover is free: museum nights, outdoor movies, festivals and markets. We tag them so they're easy to find. See [free things to do](/guides/free-things-to-do/).

## Volunteer

United Way of Northwest Arkansas and the Northwest Arkansas Food Bank are good first stops. Both list ways to help, from one-time shifts to ongoing roles. See [Give and volunteer](/guides/#give-and-volunteer).

## Use your library

Library calendars are full of clubs, classes and talks, all free. See [Libraries](/guides/#libraries).

## Get involved in your city

City boards and commissions need residents, and council meetings are open to everyone. Your city hall's site lists both.

## Tell us what we missed

If something is opening, closing or happening and we haven't covered it, [send us a tip](/submit/).
